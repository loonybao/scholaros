## Description

We are recruiting one
postdoctoral researcher
to join our team.
Your role
You will define and lead a research agenda at the intersection of multimodal large language models and networking, examples include semantic and context-aware networking, resource management in edge computing systems, and AI-native network control. An important part of the role is designing and building realistic simulators to generate data, evaluate algorithms, and study network behavior under dynamic conditions. Our ongoing project funded through NSF and RCF joint project provides a motivating public-safety communication context.
You will work closely with the PI and with international collaborators, and will take a leading role in mentoring PhD and Master's students while advancing your independent research profile.
The position requires
PhD in computer science, telecommunications / electrical engineering, or a related field
Excellent research track record in networking or MLLMs or LLMs
Demonstrated experience in building and designing simulation environments
Merit: Experience in developing algorithms for resource management
Ability to provide guidance and mentorship to doctoral researchers and master’s students, and collaborate with diverse research groups
What we offer
You will become a part of the Cloud and Network Computing group, headed by assistant professor Gopika Premsankar. Our group is dedicated to advancing research in networked systems, primarily focused on lowering the environmental footprint of such systems while enhancing performance and reliability. The NSF-RCF joint project provides opportunities for visiting collaborators in North Carolina State University and Penn State University. The role is fully funded for 2 years, with the possibility of a longer stay.
You will receive a competitive salary, starting at approximately 4220 EUR / month for a postdoctoral researcher. The contract includes comprehensive occupational health benefits.
Application documents
To apply, please share the following application materials with us through our recruitment site ("Apply now!” button at the bottom of the page):
A CV
A short letter of motivation, at most 1 page, which describes your interest and motivation (please try to include details that are not part of your CV and directly address the requirements listed above)
Sample code for simulations, preferably on a code repository
Names and email addresses of those who could provide a reference letter (at least two are required). They will be contacted directly at a later stage in the application process.
Publication list with 1 or 2 key publications highlighted. At the end of the list, please describe in one paragraph the relevance of the highlighted publication(s) to the role.
Application process
