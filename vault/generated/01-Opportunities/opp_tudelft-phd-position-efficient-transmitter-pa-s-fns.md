## Description

PhD position Enabling highly efficient and scalable transmitter arrays through built-in self-test (BIST), self-calibration, intelligent linearization techniques.
Job description
The Ph.D. project will focus on the development of novel IC architectures and algorithms enabling integrated self-test and self-calibration techniques for mm-wave and sub-mm-wave transmitters. The research will investigate sensing, monitoring, and calibration concepts that can be embedded within the stringent area and power constraints of advanced CMOS technology nodes.
In addition, the research will address the calibration and linearization challenges associated with future 6G transmitters, including spectral purity, output power, and modulation linearity requirements. Novel adaptive compensation and embedded digital predistortion techniques will be investigated to mitigate process-induced variability and dynamic load modulation effects arising in densely integrated antenna array architectures.
The work combines RF, analog, mixed-signal, and digital IC design, contributing to the realization of intelligent and energy-efficient transmitter solutions for next-generation wireless communication systems.
You will work in the ELCA research group of the TU Delft and will be guided by Dr.Marco Spirito and Dr. Morteza Alavi.
