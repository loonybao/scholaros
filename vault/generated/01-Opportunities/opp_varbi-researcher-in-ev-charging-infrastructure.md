Researcher in EV charging infrastructure
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
Job description
The electrification of heavy-duty transportation requires next-generation charging infrastructure capable of delivering fast, efficient, and reliable charging while supporting future grid services through vehicle-to-grid (V2G) technologies and mobile energy storage.
This project focuses on the design, modeling, control, and hardware development of fast charging systems for heavy-duty electric vehicles. The research covers high-power power electronic converters, system-level modeling, control strategies, grid integration, the coordination of mobile energy storage.
This project is funded by Sweden's Innovation Agency, in collaboration with City of Stockholm, Hitachi Energy, Vattenfall, etc.
