Job description
Would you like to build the scientific foundation for Swedish nitrocellulose? We are looking for a postdoc at the competence centre Svenskt Krut at KTH, where academia, government agencies and industry jointly strengthen Sweden's capability in nitrocellulose (NC).
In the project we map how the properties of the cellulose feedstock affect the nitrocellulose that can be produced. Your role is to characterise different cellulose feedstocks – including fibre structure, crystallinity, purity, accessibility and water retention – and relate these parameters to the suitability of the raw materials.
You will plan and carry out systematic small-scale nitration trials under controlled and safe conditions, analyse the resulting NC with the centre's analysis package and quantify the relationship between feedstock and NC properties. The work is carried out at the Department of Fibre and Polymer Technology and in close collaboration with FOI, including at least one longer stay there.
