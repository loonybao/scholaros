## Description

Job description
What happens when the Internet fails?
What happens when a submarine cable is cut? When satellites lose contact with one another? Or when a cyberattack disrupts the networks we depend on every day? The Internet is remarkably resilient, but it is far from invulnerable. As our dependence on digital infrastructure grows, so does the need to understand where it is vulnerable, how failures spread, and what we can do about them.
These are some of the questions we want to explore in RESILIOUS.
We are bringing together 15 PhD researchers and a network of universities, research centres, and industry partners across Europe to investigate how we can make the Internet more resilient. The research spans satellite communications, underwater robotics, network security, autonomous systems, and Internet governance. As part of RESILIOUS, you'll have the opportunity to explore challenging research questions, experiment with new ideas, and work alongside researchers and industry experts from different disciplines and countries. We are recruiting 15 PhD students. If you are curious about how the Internet works, why it sometimes fails, and what we could do differently, have a look at our open positions. One of these questions might be yours to explore.
The 15 PhD Positions offered are as follows:
DC1 — Can satellite networks stay connected when things go wrong?
DC2 — What if satellites from different operators could talk to each other?
DC3 — Can we make the Internet more resilient by rethinking how networks connect?
DC4 — Doctoral candidate in predictive robotic inspection for resilient undersea cable infrastructure
DC5 — How can we detect cyber anomalies in space systems?
DC6 — How close is too close when satellites meet in orbit?
DC7 — What are our options when satellite navigation stops working?
DC8 — Could mobile networks help us detect drones in the sky?
DC9 — Can we hear a maritime threat before we can see it?
DC10 — Can satellite networks autonomously adapt their routes when conditions change?
DC11 — Can we predict when a communication link is about to degrade and proactively mitigate its impact on connectivity?
DC12 — How can context-aware knowledge enable autonomous network self-healing?
DC13 — How do underwater networks stay connected when links disappear?
DC14 — Who decides how the Internet should work?
DC15 — Can the Internet become fragile because of how it is governed?

About the programme
The 15 PhD positions are offered within RESILIOUS (Building Europe’s Internet Resilience for a Connected Future), a European Doctoral Network funded by the European Union under the Horizon Europe Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA).
