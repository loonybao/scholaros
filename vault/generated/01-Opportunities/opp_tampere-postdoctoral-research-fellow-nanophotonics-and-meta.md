Joining

Chips for Europe Initiative

Within this vibrant setting, the rapidly expanding MetaPhotonics Group explores how light can be controlled at the nanoscale through metasurfaces, nonlinear nanophotonics, and quantum light–matter interactions. The successful applicant will play a key role in advancing one or more of the following research directions: high harmonic generation in nanostructured solids with key applications in extreme ultraviolet light sources, nanoscale optical isolators for chip-scale logic, nanoresonators for single-photon sources, and entangled two-photon absorption for quantum photochemistry with applications in photodynamic therapy.
As part of our team, you will have the opportunity to:
Contribute to research at the forefront of nanophotonics.
Work with state-of-the-art nanofabrication facilities, advanced optical laboratories, and high-performance computing infrastructure.
Receive mentorship tailored to your career goals, including opportunities for supervision, grant applications, teaching, and industry engagement.
We are seeking a highly motivated
postdoctoral researcher
with a strong background in optics, photonics or a closely related field. The successful candidate will demonstrate:
A PhD in Physics, Optics, Photonics, Engineering, or a related discipline, or a doctoral thesis submitted for examination.
Relevant experience in one or more of the following areas: cleanroom nanofabrication, optical measurement techniques, or computational design of nanophotonic components.
A proven ability to conduct original research independently, evidenced by scholarly outputs such as peer‑reviewed publications.
Potential to develop innovative research ideas and translate them into competitive funding applications, including individual fellowships.
Strong interpersonal and communication skills, with the ability to mentor junior researchers and contribute to productive collaborations.
