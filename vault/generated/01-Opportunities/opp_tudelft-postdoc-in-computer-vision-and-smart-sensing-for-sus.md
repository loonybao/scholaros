Job description
Aluminium is one of the world's most widely used metals, and its recycling plays a vital role in sustainable metallurgy and a circular economy. However, current aluminium recycling processes face significant challenges, particularly in recovering and sorting aluminium scraps from complex and heterogeneous waste streams. High-quality recycling increasingly depends on advanced sensing technologies that can accurately characterise scrap properties and identify alloy types, enabling more efficient downstream metallurgical processing and improved metal circularity.
Over recent decades, numerous sensing technologies have been developed for metal scrap characterization. Yet, robust and cost-effective solutions suitable for industrial deployment remain limited. Next-generation recycling systems will combine multiple sensing techniques (e.g., optical sensing, spectroscopy, and electromagnetic sensing) with artificial intelligence (AI) to robustly deliver high-quality recycled aluminium for promoting circularity.
The Engineering Structures (ES) Department at TU Delft is seeking a motivated Postdoctoral Researcher to advance smart sensing and AI technologies for sustainable aluminium recycling as part of the Horizon Europe CLEANMAT (Smart Clean Aluminium Material Processing) project. CLEANMAT brings together seventeen partners from eight countries, spanning universities, research and technology organisations, industrial companies, SMEs and industrial associations. The project aims to establish a low-emission, end-to-end pathway for converting challenging scrap streams into high-performance aluminium alloys.
CLEANMAT — About the Horizon Europe Project on Clean Aluminium Material Processing
