Project description
Third-cycle subject:
Applied Physics or Biotechnology
Hormones shape metabolism, fertility, mood and sleep, yet they are rarely measured, and then mostly by blood tests in a clinic. In the Hormones Project, funded by KTH InspireLab, we are developing an affordable optical system that measures six hormones in urine, at home, every day. As a doctoral student you will work at the border between biophysics and biotechnology: you will prepare and characterise urine samples, validate against established laboratory methods, and design and test the detection system.
The project is jointly led by three research leaders at AlbaNova and SciLifeLab. Read more about the project
here
