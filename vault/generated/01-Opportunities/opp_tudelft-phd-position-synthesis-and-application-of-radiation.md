## Description

Job description
As part of a large national consortium aimed at the development of nanoparticle-based immunotherapy for cancer treatment, you will design, synthesize and characterize radiation-sensitive block copolymers that self-assemble into micelles, and test their ability to encapsulate and release immunotherapeutic agents. You will develop a library of block copolymers with variations in polymer block length and structure, as well as the radiation-sensitive linker. You will assess the efficiency of radiation-induced polymer degradation and cargo release, as well as initial cell studies. The project will be carried out as a collaboration between the groups of prof. Rienk Eelkema (Chemical Engineering) and prof. Antonia Denkova (Radiation Science and Technology).
Job requirements
You have an MSc degree in chemistry, chemical engineering or equivalent
You have proven expertise in multistep organic synthesis
You have experience in purification and characterization of organic molecules
Experience in polymer synthesis, polymer characterization and physical chemistry are desirable
You work well in a team
You have a high level of written and spoken English
