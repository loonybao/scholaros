## Description

Aalto University is home to researchers working at the frontier of artificial intelligence. Some of that work has the potential to become more than research — to address real problems, create new industries, and contribute to Finland's deep tech future. The Innovation Postdoc exists to support that transition.
This is a 12-month funded position for doctoral researchers and postdocs who have a concrete result, technology, or invention with commercial potential, and who are ready to dedicate their full focus to exploring it. You will work on validating your technology with real users and partners, developing a business model, and preparing for the next step — whether that is external investment, a spinout, or another commercialisation pathway — supported by Aalto's innovation infrastructure, coaching network, and peer community.
No prior business experience is required. What we are looking for is research with genuine differentiation, and a researcher with the drive to find out what it can become.
What you will do
As an ‘Innovation Postdoc’, your work is 100% focused on increasing the
innovation readiness level
of your research. Your responsibilities include:
Conducting structured problem and market validation through customer discovery interviews and stakeholder engagement
Developing technology if needed – with ‘product’ in mind.
Testing and iterating your technology with real-world users and partners
Developing a compelling business model and go-to-market strategy
Preparing for external investment, spinout formation, or other commercialization pathways
Participating in the Innovation Postdoc program activities, coaching sessions, and peer community (eg trade fair, Business Generator workshops, some events at The Founder House Helsinki etc.)
Who we are looking for
You do not need a business background, but you must be ready to adapt. We are looking for researchers with the mindset of a founder.
Required:
Doctoral degree (PhD) completed or submitted the thesis, from Aalto University
You have a doctoral degree in other universities, but working at Aalto University as a postdoctoral researcher
A concrete research result, technology, or invention with a clear differentiation and commercial potential in the corresponding field
Demonstrated ability to work independently and drive projects forward under uncertainty
We value attitudes like:
Obsession with a real-world problem, not just the technology – you are driven by the desire to solve something that matters
Coachability and open-mindedness – you are ready to step outside the academic bubble, actively seek feedback from customers and mentors, and change course when the evidence demands it
Adaptability and resilience – you understand that building a deep-tech spinout is a journey of uncertainty, and you treat setbacks as data, not failure
Business curiosity – you do not need a degree, but you are genuinely interested in how value is created and captured. You think about markets, customers, and competition, not just technical performance.
Eagerness for impact – you have a technology with strong differentiation and are motivated to see it applied and scaled in the real world.
‘can-do’ attitude – you are ready to figure out what you don't know, roll up your sleeves, and get things done even without a clear roadmap
