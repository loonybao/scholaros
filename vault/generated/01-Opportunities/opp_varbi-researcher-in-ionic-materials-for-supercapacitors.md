Job description
The driver is the neeed for material science to provide sustainable solutons in energy production. The position is concerned with research into the design of a novel supercapacitor based on the self-assembly of ionic materials at interfaces. It will span research from understanding properties of ionic liquids and their solutions, the role of structure and charge, and the response to surface potential.
The succesful applicant will be responsible for driving a proof of concept project, performing the necessary experiments, interpreting them and writing reports and scientific publications in high ranked journals. The job will also entail coordinating input from a multidisciplinary team and designing a supercapacitor demonstrator.
The School of Engineering Sciences in Chemistry, Biotechnology and Health CBH conducts research and education in areas that cover several global challenges. Among them are the need of sustainable energy, health and aging population, climate change, sustainable production and working life, food production and clean water. It consists of 9 units, amongst which The Chemistry Department is one of the largest. Research and teaching are very diverse but are united by a molecular approach. We work on important research problems with broad relevance for energy, materials, environment and health, and a common theme is materials and molecules at interfaces. Surface and Corrosion Science is one of 4 divisions within the department.
