## Description

Study how passengers experience automated buses and help evaluate a new service connecting Delft Campus station and the TU Delft campus.
Job description
This postdoc position is part of an international project aimed at automated bus transport. Automated buses have proven they can drive safely, but most still require onboard staff because supervision, safety assurance, and regulatory requirements must be met before driverless operation is possible. The project aims to remove this barrier by combining remote supervision, passenger safety monitoring, and fleet management into a single operational framework. With demonstrations in Switzerland, the Netherlands, and the United Kingdom, the project aims to create deployment-ready solutions that can be scaled after 2030. Automated bus transport is essential to maintain and expand public transport services, enabling operators to deliver more public transport while supporting accessible and zero-emission mobility. For the demonstration in the Netherlands, the project plans to study an automated bus service connecting the train station Delft Campus to the TU Delft premises.
As postdoctoral researcher you will focus on the acceptance of automated buses. To apply vehicle automation in public transport at a larger scale, it is essential to carefully assess the acceptance by end users, understand the contribution of vehicle automation to the overall public transport system (for instance as first/last kilometer) and evaluate the lessons learned on the demonstration sites. Specific tasks are: (1) definition of the impact assessment and evaluation plan, (2) conducting research into user acceptance and positioning in the public transport system, (3) consolidate lessons learned from the demonstration sites and (4) produce a final dissemination report. Specific emphasis will be on the demonstration site in the Netherlands, working with DAM Shuttles and supported by the concession provider MRDH, the city of Delft and public transport operators RET and HTM. As postdoctoral researcher you will also write scientific papers and present results at conferences.
At TU Delft you will be part of the Sustainable Urban Multimodal mobility (SUM) Lab in the Department of Transport & Planning.
