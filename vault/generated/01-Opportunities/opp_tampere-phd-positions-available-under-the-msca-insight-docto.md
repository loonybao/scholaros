## Description

Are you interested in pursuing a fully funded PhD in Europe and contributing to research in optical communications, photonics, artificial intelligence and advanced sensing technologies?
Applications are now open for 15 fully funded Doctoral Candidate positions under INSIGHT, a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network funded by the European Union's Horizon Europe programme.
The INSIGHT network brings together academic institutions and industry partners across Europe to train researchers in Optical Integrated Sensing and Communication (O-ISAC). The programme aims to develop technologies that allow optical fibre networks to support high-speed data transmission and environmental sensing simultaneously.
Researchers from around the world, including India, are welcome to apply, provided they meet the eligibility requirements.
About the INSIGHT Doctoral Network
INSIGHT is a 36-month international and intersectoral doctoral training programme that combines research in optical communications, distributed fibre sensing, photonic integration, digital signal processing, machine learning and network optimisation.
Each selected doctoral candidate will work on an individual research project while participating in a coordinated European training network.
The programme offers opportunities to collaborate with academic and industrial researchers, undertake international secondments, attend scientific conferences and develop skills in research communication, innovation, entrepreneurship and intellectual property.

Research Areas
The available projects cover several research topics, including:
Optical communications and fibre sensing: Developing systems that combine high-capacity data transmission with distributed sensing.
Photonic integration and frequency combs: Investigating advanced optical technologies for communication and sensing networks.
Artificial intelligence and machine learning: Applying computational methods to optical signal reconstruction, anomaly detection and network optimisation.
Environmental and infrastructure monitoring: Developing sensing technologies for structural health, temperature changes and environmental conditions.
Advanced signal processing: Improving sensing sensitivity, noise tolerance and communication performance.
Applicants can explore the individual project descriptions, research objectives and host-specific requirements through the official recruitment portal.

Eligibility Criteria
The INSIGHT Doctoral Network welcomes applicants of all nationalities, including researchers from India.
To be eligible, candidates must meet the following requirements.
Academic qualifications: Applicants must hold a master's degree or equivalent qualification that provides access to doctoral education before employment and enrolment. Relevant disciplines include physics, photonics, optics, electrical and communication engineering, computer science, data science, applied mathematics and related fields.
Doctoral status: Applicants must not hold a doctoral degree at the date of recruitment.
MSCA mobility rule: Candidates must not have resided or carried out their main activity (work or studies) in the country of the recruiting organisation for more than 12 months during the 36 months immediately before recruitment, subject to the applicable MSCA rules and exceptions.
Language requirements: Applicants must demonstrate sufficient spoken and written English to undertake doctoral research and scientific communication. Language certification is required only where specified by the degree-awarding institution.
Candidates must also be willing to relocate to the recruiting institution and participate in international secondments and network training activities.
