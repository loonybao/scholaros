## Description

The
QD group
is looking for a highly motivated
Postdoctoral Researcher
to work on advances in controlling the emission properties of organic light-emitting diodes using nanoparticles embedded within the device. The work combines advanced nanofabrication methods, such as electron-beam lithography and OLED fabrication via evaporation and solution-processing with sophisticated characterization methods such as Fourier plane imaging. The project has a concurrent goal to commercialize the results of the project.
Your role and goals
The successful candidate is expected to develop novel nanoparticle array geometries for improved OLED device performance.  The tasks include design, fabrication and experimental characterisation of the OLED devices. The fabrication utilizes wide departmental infrastructures and a state-of-the-art cleanroom, Micronova, and characterization is carried out in the QD group's optics lab with advanced experimental instruments. We are searching for a Postdoctoral Researcher with strong hands-on experimental skills and a deep understanding of organic electronics and nanophotonics.
The work is done in close collaboration with theorists of the group, who study the nanostructures and device optimization using simulations. The goal is to develop precise control of specific emission properties such as polarization, color, and angular distribution while simultaneously extracting lossy modes of the devices and to gain advanced understanding of the system.  We aim to commercialize the results of this research with a focus on significantly increasing the efficiency of OLEDs while simultaneously offering precise control over the emission properties.
Your experience and ambitions
We are looking for a bright and motivated Postdoctoral Researcher with an excellent record. Hands-on experience in nano- and OLED fabrication, characterization and electronics is required. The applicant should hold (or expect soon) a PhD degree in a suitable field. Good command of English (both verbal and written) and demonstrated ability to disseminate scientific results are mandatory requirements for the position. Finnish language is not required. Applicants must be motivated to conduct research at the highest international level.
