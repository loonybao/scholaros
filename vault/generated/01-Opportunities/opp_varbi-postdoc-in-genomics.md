Job description
The Swedish National Genomics Infrastructure is looking for a postdoctoral researcher for a project aiming to leverage state-of-the-art genomics to study links between gene regulatory variants, chromatin organization and transcription. The project will combine hands-on genomics, technology development and computational biology.
The project will be conducted at the interphase between the National Genomics Infrastructure and the research groups of Professor Tuuli Lappalainen and Associate professor Robert Månsson. This bring together, state-of-the-art genomics platforms, international forefront of functional genomics, molecular biology and experimental hematology.
