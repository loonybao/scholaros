## Description

Integration of heating and cooling sources and demand.
Job description
At present, heating and cooling represents around 50% of the final energy use in Europe and is mainly supplied by fossil fuel derived energy. The performance of sustainable heating and cooling supply systems is tightly coupled to the heating and cooling demand. In this postdoctoral researcher project you will contribute to the modelling of energy systems integrating subsurface energy extraction and storage systems with the buildings which they supply. In addition, you will contribute to teaching on the Sustainable Energy Technology Programme.
You will work on two externally funded projects, one related to energy extraction from in-land waterways via energy geostructures and the second on the performance of ATES energy storage systems. In both projects, integration of the systems where the heat is produced and used is key. Hence, accurate simulation of production temperature and subsurface processes affecting this is a key task to be able to control and optimise system performance. In both projects, the collection of data, system modelling and validation are the key research methods. The validated models are then used to assess how all components function together under various load conditions and control schemes, and how their performance can be optimised. You will assess the energy flexibility and reliability of the system under varying energy demand conditions over the lifetime of the building(s) supplied.
Activities.
In this PD you will contribute to various aspects of modelling and integrating various energy systems:
Input into the coordination and organisation of the activities at experimental sites on the Delft campus, with a focus on ensuring monitoring systems retrieve needed data for model validation.
Investigate the performance of the project by carrying out modelling and comparing with field data, with a focus on (but not limited to) the subsurface temperature and flow performance.
Integrating existing individual component models into energy system models. Modelling strategies and integration approaches and interfaces will need to be developed in order to reduce the computational load.
Investigate how system integration affects subsurface energy storage performance and how control options can influence overall system performance.
Support education in the MSc Sustainable Energy Technology programme by giving lectures, supporting tutorials and guiding thesis projects.
