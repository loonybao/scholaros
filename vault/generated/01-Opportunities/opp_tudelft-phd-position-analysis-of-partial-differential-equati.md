## Description

Explore the mathematics of water waves: join TU Delft as a PhD candidate and investigate nonlinear wave phenomena through analysis of PDEs.
Job description
The Delft Institute for Applied Mathematics (DIAM) at Delft University of Technology (TU Delft) invites applications for a full-time PhD position in the field of analysis of partial differential equations (PDEs). The PhD candidate will be supervised by Dr. Anna Geyer.
The research will focus on the analysis of PDEs in the context of water waves and nonlinear dispersive equations. The project aims to advance the mathematical understanding of complex wave phenomena, drawing on techniques from PDE methods, functional analysis, dynamical systems, modelling and asymptotic analysis. Potential topics include stability theory for nonlinear PDEs, the existence and stability of solutions with non-smooth profiles, instability and wave breaking.
For more insights into the research, please visit A. Geyer’s webpage https://annageyer.wordpress.com
Throughout the PhD, the candidate will receive support in developing new mathematical skills and deepening and broadening their expertise. The candidate will have the opportunity to connect with an international research network through active participation in conferences and research visits, for which a travel budget is available.
The appointment is for a duration of 48 months, starting between spring and fall of 2027
The position offers the opportunity to gain teaching experience through a light teaching load. The working language will be English. If desired by the candidate, there is the possiblity to take Dutch language courses offered by the Language Center for Languages and Academic Skills of TU Delft, see https://www.tudelft.nl/en/tpm/itav/education/dutch-courses/courses.
