We are looking for a
Postdoctoral Research Fellow
to develop new theories and methodologies for Deep Learning.
The research scope is intentionally open. The specific research topic will be selected jointly with each successful applicant based on:
the scientific importance and potential impact of the topic,
its relevance to emerging directions in Machine Learning and related domains (including Computer Vision, Robot Perception, AIoT, Neuromorphic Computing, etc.)
the applicant’s background, strengths, and research interests, and
its fit with the research activities of the Computational Intelligence group and the broader ELLIS research community.
The positions are funded by
ELLIS Institute Finland
, which places Fundamental Machine Learning research at the core of its activities and connects this research with applications and societal impact. The successful applicants will join the Computational Intelligence group led by Professor Alexandros Iosifidis at Tampere University.
The position is full-time and will be filled for a fixed term of three years, starting on 1 February 2027, or as soon as possible thereafter by mutual agreement. The workplace is located at the Hervanta Campus of Tampere University, Finland.
The Postdoctoral Research Fellow will interact closely with ELLIS Institute Finland and the wider ELLIS community. ELLIS is a pan-European network of excellence in machine learning and AI that supports international collaboration and the development of researchers across Europe.
JOB DESCRIPTION
The Postdoctoral Research Fellow will conduct ambitious research on the
theoretical foundations and methodological development of deep learning models
.
The research may address, but is not limited to:
learning principles and theoretical foundations of deep models,
representation learning and self-supervised learning,
optimization and training dynamics of deep neural networks,
generalization, robustness, adaptation, and uncertainty,
efficient, structured, modular, or interpretable deep learning,
multimodal, generative, or foundation models,
continual, collaborative, or distributed learning,
deep learning for scientific, physical, or embodied systems, and
other high-impact or emerging topics in machine learning.
These areas are indicative rather than prescriptive. Applicants are encouraged to propose original research questions that they consider important, timely, and scientifically ambitious.
The successful applicant will be expected to pursue substantial methodological or theoretical contributions rather than only apply established models to new datasets or application domains. Application-driven research is welcome when it motivates or requires new machine learning methods, theories, or learning principles.
The Postdoctoral Research Fellow will:
define and lead an original research agenda,
formulate important research questions in deep learning,
develop and analyze new learning methods and models,
conduct theoretically or empirically rigorous research,
publish results in leading international conferences and journals,
present research at major international scientific events,
collaborate with researchers at the Computational Intelligence group, its collaborators, and ELLIS Institute Finland,
contribute to research proposals and the development of new collaborative projects,
contribute to the supervision and mentoring of doctoral and master’s students, and
take an active role in the scientific activities of the Computational Intelligence group and the ELLIS Institute Finland.
The research is expected to target internationally leading venues relevant to the selected topic. These may include NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AISTATS, COLT, JMLR, AAAI, IJCAI, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, CoRL, IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence, and other venues included in the
ELLIS list of approved publication venues
.
In addition, the duties of the Postdoctoral Research Fellow may include some teaching tasks (5-10%) in topics connected to their expertise.
REQUIREMENTS
Applicants must have:
A doctoral degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Signal Processing, Robotics, or another relevant field.
A strong publication record in leading peer-reviewed conferences or journals in machine learning, artificial intelligence, or a closely related field.
At least one publication in an
ELLIS-approved publication venue
, or a publication of comparable scientific quality and selectivity in a directly relevant field.
Evidence of a substantial personal contribution to the publications included in the application.
Strong programming skills and experience with at least one major deep learning framework, such as PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow.
The ability to lead research independently while collaborating effectively in an international research environment, and
The ability to communicate research clearly through scientific publications, presentations, and discussions.
The doctoral degree must have been completed before the position begins. Applicants who have submitted their doctoral dissertation but have not yet formally received the degree may apply, provided that they can complete all degree requirements before the agreed starting date.
