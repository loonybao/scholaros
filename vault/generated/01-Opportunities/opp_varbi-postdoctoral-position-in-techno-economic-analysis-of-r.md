## Description

Postdoctoral position in techno-economic analysis of resilient urban energy systems
Login and apply
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Industrial Engineering and Management
Do you want to develop and evaluate concepts for how a brand-new urban district can be supplied with both heat and electricity robustly, even when something unexpected happens, and see your analyses used as decision support by the municipality that will build it? Welcome with your application.
The position is part of a collaboration between KTH, Uppsala University and Uppsala Municipality on the energy system in Uppsala’s south-eastern districts. This is one of Sweden’s largest urban development projects, with 21,500 homes planned by 2050. You will work on a real question in a live setting, where your results directly affect how a district is built.
Two postdocs
are being recruited in parallel to work closely together:
one at KTH
(this position) and
one at Uppsala University. Part of the working time is spent at Uppsala Municipality
, which gives direct access to planning documentation, stakeholders and the questions that are current during implementation. The project is part of the research area STandUP for Energy.
Job description
The position aims to generate and evaluate concepts for the energy supply of Uppsala’s south-eastern districts, and to analyse them techno-economically as decision support for the municipality.
Duties include:
developing, modelling and comparing concepts for a flexible low-temperature heating system
techno-economically evaluating a flexible electricity microgrid
analysing the heating system and the electricity microgrid as an integrated multi-energy system
quantifying the socio-economic value of the energy system’s resilience
analysing the security of supply of the heat and electricity systems during disruptions, and how concept and sizing choices affect recovery time and outage costs
developing scenarios for the district’s build-out, connection rates, energy demand, technology rollout and system configuration
carrying out techno-economic analyses of the integrated system
analysing business models and implementation models for both systems
translating the results into decision support
publishing results in scientific journals and presenting them at conferences
taking part in applications for external research funding, nationally and within the EU
