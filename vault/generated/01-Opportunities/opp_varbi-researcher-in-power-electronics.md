Job description
The energy system is undergoing a fundamental transformation driven by the rapid integration of renewable energy, electric vehicles and large-scale AI data centers. Power electronic converters play a pivotal role in enabling this transition, while also introducing new challenges related to system dynamics, stability, and control.
This project focuses on the development of modeling, stability analysis, control, and hardware solutions for converter-dominated power systems. Application areas include next-generation megawatt charging infrastructure for heavy-duty electric vehicles and power electronic systems supporting AI data centers. The project is funded by EU Clean Energy Transition Partnership and Swedish Energy Agency. The recruited researcher will join the Division of Electric Power and Energy Systems at KTH Royal Institute of Technology, working closely with world-leading power electronic groups in University of Padova, Aalborg University, Chalmers University of Technology , and with a set of industrial partners, i.e., Hitachi Energy and Energiforsk, the system operators Svenska kraftnät, Volvo Energy.
