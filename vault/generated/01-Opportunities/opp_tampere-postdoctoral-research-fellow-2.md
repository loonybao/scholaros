## Description

Tampere University and Tampere University of Applied Sciences create a unique environment for multidisciplinary, inspirational and high-impact research and education. Our universities community has its competitive edges in technology, health and society.
www.tuni.fi/en
The research group of Wireless Systems
at the
Electrical Engineering Unit
at the
Faculty of Information Technology and Communication Sciences
at Tampere University focuses on next-generation wireless communication systems and standards. Our research spans fundamental theory, algorithm design, system-level analysis, and practical implementations for 6G and beyond wireless technologies. We work on a broad range of topics, including advanced physical layer design, multiple-antenna systems, reconfigurable intelligent surfaces, integrated sensing and communications, non-terrestrial and UAV networks, physical layer security, quantum-enabled communications, semantic and AI-native wireless systems, and energy-efficient/low-power communication. Our vision is to develop disruptive wireless technologies that enable secure, intelligent, sustainable, and highly connected future networks for terrestrial, aerial, space, industrial, and defense applications.
In Autumn 2026, we will start a 4-year Research Council of Finland project “NOISY: Toward Noise-Driven Communication for Secure, Energy-Efficient, and Low-Complexity Wireless Networks”. The NOISY project explores a bold idea: what if noise, usually seen as a disturbance, could actually be used to send information? By harnessing natural and artificial noise sources, the project aims to build wireless systems that are secure, energy-efficient, and simple: key goals for future 6G networks and the Internet of Things. Using mathematical modeling, computer simulations, and practical experiments with software-defined radios, the research will take place at Tampere Wireless Research Center. The results could enable ultra-low-power communication, protect data from eavesdropping, and support sustainable digitalization.
JOB DESCRIPTION
We are now looking for a Postdoctoral Research Fellow
to join our team and contribute to the project NOISY, which develops new noise-driven communication methods for future 6G and IoT systems. The work will focus on theoretical modeling, signaling, and algorithm design, simulations, and practical validation of low-power, low-complexity, and secure wireless communication concepts. Depending on the applicant's expertise and interests, the work may include:

Designing and evaluating low-power and low-complexity signaling schemes for future IoT and 6G systems.
Studying secure and covert communication approaches based on noise-like signals.
Performing analytical work, simulations, and performance comparisons.
Contributing to laboratory proof-of-concept demonstrations using SDR platforms when relevant.
Preparing high-quality scientific publications and contributing to project reporting and dissemination.
Participation in project management, mentoring of Ph.D. students, and supervision of thesis projects is expected. The job requires occasional travel to international conferences to disseminate research results. International research visits to other universities or research institutes are also possible.
