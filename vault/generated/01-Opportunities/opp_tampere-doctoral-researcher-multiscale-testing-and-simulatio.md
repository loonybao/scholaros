Within the unit of Materials Science and Environmental Engineering, faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences, we are recruiting one (1) doctoral position. Job description
We are looking for one (1) Doctoral Researcher in the areas of fibre reinforced composites, experimental testing and multi-scale numerical analysis. Successful candidates will pursue a doctoral degree at Tampere University. Full-time doctoral studies are expected to be completed in three to four years.
You will focus on the development of a universal numerical and empirical methodology to determine full-scale composite properties using the input data from microscale tests. The visioned methodology focuses on (i) collecting microscale interfacial adhesion data based on high throughput microbond tests, (ii) devising mesoscale composite specimen and test geometries to bridge the gap between micro- and macroscale mechanical tests, and (iii) developing numerical procedures to enable the virtual assessment of composites structures. In addition, the project will include developing novel fibre surface treatments and manufacturing of composite specimens in collaboration with academic partners. Thus, in this position you will have the opportunity to undertake short-term research visits outside Finland during the project.
The work is done for the project funded by Research Council of Finland (Scalable composite analysis through linked experimental and simulation techniques - SCALES). The objective of the project is to generate a methodology that enables to deduce the full-scale properties of a composite structure by using the data from micro-scale mechanical tests and multi-scale numerical analysis. As a result, this project will enable faster, safer and more affordable material development and results in novel material innovations for improved performance and sustainability.
As a Doctoral Researcher you are expected to perform overviews of state of the art, to study the principles of mechanics of composites and their testing, modelling and simulation. You are also expected to develop the computational approaches, to generate experimental results, to analyse and validate them on use case(s) through comparison with the state of the art.
The results of your research will be disseminated in scientific peer-reviewed journals, international conferences, and events with industry. The tasks of doctoral researchers also include teaching duties that amount to approximately 5% of their annual working hours, unless there are justified reasons to the contrary.
