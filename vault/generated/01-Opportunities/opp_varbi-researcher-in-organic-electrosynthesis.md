Researcher in organic electrosynthesis
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Engineering Sciences in Chemistry, Biotechnology and Health
Job description
We seek a driven research engineer to carry out method development in organic electrosynthesis in the research group of Associate Professor Helena Lundberg at the Department of Chemistry. Administrative tasks are also important aspects of the work.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree in the subject area.
Documented ability to work independently as well as collaborative in a group.
Documented scientific skills in method development for organic electrosynthesis.
The ability to skillfully communicate in English, verbally and in writing, as it is required in the daily work.
Personal qualities
As a person, you possess academic skills (both practical laboratory skills and theoretical), you are communicative and show good collaboration skills. Furthermore, you are independent in your work and have good pedagogical skills.
Preferred qualifications
Experience of administering chemical inventory.
Awareness of diversity and equal treatment policies and practices.
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
