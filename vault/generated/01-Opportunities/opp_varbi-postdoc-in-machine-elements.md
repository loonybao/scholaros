Job description
We are looking for a postdoctoral fellow who wants to contribute to the next generation of energy-efficient and sustainable transport systems through world-class research in tribology and machine elements.
Friction losses in vehicle systems still account for a significant portion of global energy use. In heavy vehicles alone, around 180 billion liters of fuel are consumed annually due to friction, while the transport sector is facing significantly stricter climate requirements. The EU's target of a 90% reduction in CO₂ emissions from new heavy vehicles by 2040 places completely new demands on energy efficiency and innovative technical solutions.
In this project, the focus is on an often overlooked but very promising component: grease-lubricated wheel bearing systems. The project aims to develop and implement a systematic and realistic method for evaluating new grease formulations, using a specially developed test rig that will be designed and built specifically for this purpose.
The project is carried out in close collaboration with leading players along the entire value chain, including Traton/Scania, Europe's leading grease manufacturer Axel Christiernsson International and KTH. The successful candidate will have a unique opportunity to work at the interface between academic research and industrial application, with direct potential to contribute to future sustainable transport solutions.
This position is part of KTH's PROCEED initiative for research activities in Södertälje, which provides a unique opportunity for research collaboration with Traton/Scania and AstraZeneca. To enable close collaboration and collaboration, regular physical presence in Södertälje is included as an important part of the position. The position offers good opportunities to build a strong international network, develop your own research and contribute to the green transition of the industry.
