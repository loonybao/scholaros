## Description

We are now inviting applications for the position of
Postdoctoral Researcher
in information economics.
The position is in the Department of Economics of Aalto University, the leading research-oriented economics unit in Finland. It is funded by the Horizon Europe ERC Starting Grant project “Modeling Misspecification”, led by Professor Daniel Hauser. The project develops new theoretical and empirical approaches to information and learning in microeconomics. The selected candidate will contribute to the project’s research, collaborate on papers and present findings, while also developing an independent research agenda.
The department focuses on theoretical and empirical microeconomics broadly defined. For more information about the Department, please visit
http://economics.aalto.fi/en/
.
The selected candidate will join the Helsinki GSE research community, participate in seminars and other research activities, and engage with graduate students. We are looking for an independent and collaborative researcher with strong analytical and communication skills.
The working language is English. Finnish language is not required. Applicants should have, or expect to have, a doctoral degree in economics or a related field by the end of 2027, and experience in microeconomic theory, experimental or survey-based research, or related methods. Candidates should be motivated by questions concerning information, learning and model misspecification.
The position is filled for two years, starting in 2027. Due to EU insurance and pension regulations, Aalto expects employees to reside and work in Finland.
What we offer
At the start of the postdoctoral career stage, the gross monthly salary is approximately 4 600 EUR/month. Aalto University provides its employees with occupational health care services and retirement benefits.
Aalto University offers its academic personnel the opportunity to teach and do research in a vibrant and active international community. We value work-life balance and well-being in all aspects of life. We work in a hybrid model, with the primary workplace located at the Otaniemi Campus in Espoo, Finland.
Application Procedure
Applications should be submitted via Aalto University’s recruitment site by 23:59 EET on January 31, 2027. Please click the "Apply now!” button on the page to apply. Application materials should include a cover letter, a CV with a list of publications and working papers, one job market paper, and names and contact information of three referees*. Please submit all application materials in English. Virtual first-round interviews will be conducted on a rolling basis starting late January 2027.
