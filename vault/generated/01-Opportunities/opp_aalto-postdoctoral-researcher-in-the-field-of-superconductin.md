## Description

Aalto University is where science and art meet technology and business. We shape a sustainable future by making research breakthroughs in and across our disciplines, sparking the game changers of tomorrow and creating novel solutions to major global challenges. Our community is made up of 16 000 students and 5 200 employees, including 446 professors. At the Department of Applied Physics, our pioneering research in physical sciences creates important industrial applications that hold great technological potential. We educate future generations of research and development professionals, data specialists, technology experts, inventors, and scientists for industry and society.
The
Kvantti group
(Superconducting Qubits and Circuit QED) at the
Department of Applied Physics
, Aalto University, is seeking outstanding candidates for the position of
Postdoctoral Researcher in experimental circuit QED
We are looking for a bright, motivated
postdoctoral researcher
to join our team. You should have excellent study records in physics, quantum engineering, or related disciplines up to the level of Ph.D.  (awarded or expected soon). You are an energetic, hands-on effective researcher who is confident to take initiative in the lab. Your main skills can be in any area of mesoscopic physics, nanoelectronics, cryogenics, microwave electronics, circuit design, clean-room techniques, simulations, data analysis, and instrument programming.
Our group is doing research on circuits consisting of superconducting qubits and resonators - presently one of the most promising platforms for future quantum computers and quantum simulators. Moreover, we explore additional applications of these devices for emerging quantum technologies such as supersensitive detection. This field of research has had a tremendous development in recent times and presently offers plenty of exciting opportunities for scientific breakthroughs.
We have several research directions in the group, with projects funded by EU, Research Council of Finland, and by Business Finland: axion detection using superconducting qubits, control of quantum computers using microwave and optical signals, and continuous-variables quantum information processing and sensing using parametric amplifiers. More details about these projects are available from the group leader. As a postdoctoral researcher you will be part of a vibrant international team striving to advance these exciting research topics, and we offer the possibility to engage in collaborations with industrial partners and other academic groups interested in quantum technologies that are part of these projects.
Your experience and ambitions
Applicants for this position should hold (or expect soon) a PhD degree in physics, electrical engineering, or computer science. Applicants should preferably have prior research experience in one or more of the following areas: design and fabrication of superconducting devices, cryogenic operation and measurements, microwave electronics, numerical simulations, quantum control, and data analysis. However, we will consider candidates with experience and expertise in other related quantum-technology fields, such as quantum optics, trapped atoms/ions, nanoelectronics, NV centers, quantum materials, etc. given that a strong motivation for transiting to a new field is demonstrated.
