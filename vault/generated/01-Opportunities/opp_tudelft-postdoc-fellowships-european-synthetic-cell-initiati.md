## Description

Join SynCell-nExUs and collaborate with leading researchers across Europe to tackle the big challenges in Synthetic Cells research.
Job description
SynCell-nExUs, (“SynCellEU programme for recruiting next generation European leaders in Synthetic Cell research”) is a new pan-European postdoctoral excellence programme led by the Max Planck Society for the Advancement of Science e.V. and managed in collaboration with the European Synthetic Cell Initiative (SynCellEU). The programme will run for five years, during which a total of 40 postdoctoral fellowships will be offered. The appointed fellows will join an interdisciplinary community of researchers dedicated to building synthetic cells from the bottom up. By recreating essential cellular functions, this community aims to better understand how living cells work and to translate this knowledge into cutting-edge, nature-inspired technologies.
In this 1st call, SynCell-nExUs offers 20 fully funded, 3-year postdoctoral fellowships.
Applicants are invited to develop their own research project in one of the following SynCell-nExUs research lines on synthetic cells:
Metabolism and energy;
Division and replication;
Information processing and evolution;
Enabling technologies.
Selected fellows will be appointed at one of the proposed hosting labs, where they will work at the own research project, while being embedded in the lab and receiving supervision by the hosting PI. At the same time, they will participate in the activities of the network. They also will have the opportunity for exchange through a personalized programme of visits and secondments. Detailed information can be found in the Application Package that can be downloaded on the SynCell-nExUs website (see link under "Application Procedure").

Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.

Excellent knowledge of the English language.
Good organisational and time-management skills.

The SynCell-nExUs programme is coordinated by the Max Planck Society and brings together a network of participating institutions across Europe. Postdoctoral researchers recruited through the programme will be hosted by one of the participating institutions, where they will carry out their research under the supervision of researchers and group leaders at the respective host institution. TU Delft is one of the participating institutions in the SynCell-nExUs programme and co-coordinates the recruitment process. Depending on the selected research project and supervisor, successful candidates may therefore be appointed at TU Delft or at another participating institution elsewhere in Europe. Candidates apply to the SynCell-nExUs programme and its research opportunities rather than exclusively to a specific host institution. The final host institution and supervisor will be determined based on the selected research project and the outcome of the recruitment process.
