ABOUT THE JOB
Are you an experimental semiconductor physicist, materials scientist or experienced R&D engineer/researcher in a closely related field excited about developing next-generation metrology instrumentation and building an international deep-tech startup from research?
ATOMEL
is a Business Finland Research-to-Business (R2B) project aimed at translating an academic innovation in scanning-probe-based thin-film characterization and electrical metrology into a new deep-tech venture serving the semiconductor industry. As modern microelectronics and advanced device architectures scale to increasingly complex ultrathin film structures, conventional metrology methods face growing limitations in resolving localized electrical and material properties.
With our mechanical and instrumentation development already underway, we are seeking an experienced Postdoctoral Researcher to take full scientific and experimental ownership of our semiconductor electrical characterization strategy, physical modeling, and benchmark validation
YOUR ROLE AND GOALS
You will join a focused, full-time R2B core team working in tandem: you (leading Semiconductor Physics & Electrical Metrology), the other postdoctoral researcher/engineer (leading high-precision instrumentation, automation and control systems), our Business Developer (commercial lead), and Dr. Ali Afzalifar (project leader). After a brief onboarding phase, you are expected to operate with a high degree of scientific autonomy, guiding the team on experimental design and measurement physics.
Key responsibilities include:
Electrical Characterization & Protocol Design:
Lead the material characterization strategy by independently designing and executing high-precision electrical measurements (such as low-current I–V, C–V, and frequency-dependent impedance/dielectric characterization) on ultrathin films and semiconductor structures.
Physical & Electrostatic Modeling:
Develop analytical physical models and numerical simulations (e.g., finite-element electrostatic and transport modeling) to predict electrical responses, evaluate substrate and interface effects, and guide experimental parameter selection.
Solid-State & Dielectric Data Interpretation:
Translate raw electrical datasets into reliable physical metrics—such as conduction and leakage mechanisms, dielectric breakdown, charge trapping, and interfacial defect states—while rigorously distinguishing true material behavior from measurement noise, parasitics, and artefacts.
Test Structure Preparation & Benchmarking:
Select, design, and prepare representative semiconductor test vehicles and reference thin films (utilizing OtaNano/Micronova cleanroom and thin-film facilities where needed) to validate and benchmark system performance against established metrology standards.
Hardware-Physics Integration & Industrial Validation:
Work closely with the instrumentation engineer to optimize electrical measurement workflows on the prototype platform, and contribute to application validation and technical discussions with industrial semiconductor partners.
YOUR EXPERIENCE AND QUALIFICATIONS
We are looking for a hands-on experimentalist who does not merely run standard commercial recipes, but understands the underlying measurement physics deeply enough to design and troubleshoot new characterization experiments from first principles.
Doctoral Degree (PhD)
: A PhD in semiconductor physics, Materials Science, Solid-State/Applied Physics, Micro-/Nanoelectronics, Electrical Engineering, or a closely related experimental field.
Advanced Electrical Characterization Expertise:
Proven track record in designing and executing high-precision electrical measurements on thin films or solid-state devices, including low-current I–V, C–V, and frequency-dependent impedance or dielectric spectroscopy.
Semiconductor & Dielectric Physics:
Strong understanding of MOS/MIS/MIM device physics, high-k dielectrics, electrical transport, dielectric breakdown, charge trapping, interface states, and low-noise/parasitic mitigation in sensitive electrical measurements.
Modeling & Quantitative Analysis:
Ability to build analytical physical models and numerical simulations (e.g., COMSOL Multiphysics, Python, or MATLAB) to interpret experimental data and guide measurement design.
Cleanroom & Thin-Film Familiarity:
Practical experience with semiconductor test structures and thin-film deposition/characterization workflows (e.g., ALD, ellipsometry, electrical SPM, or cleanroom wafer testing) is an advantage.
Entrepreneurial Drive & Communication:
Fluency in English (Finnish is not required), independent problem-solving mindset, and strong motivation to transition into the upcoming deep-tech spin-out company as a key technical leader or potential co-founder after the R2B phase.
WHAT WE OFFER
We offer a high-impact position at the intersection of frontier nanoscale science and commercial semiconductor hardware. You will be based in the Department of Applied Physics at Aalto University with direct access to world-class cleanroom and characterization infrastructure at OtaNano and Micronova (including ALD, electron microscopy, ellipsometry, and advanced electrical/SPM laboratories), alongside mentoring from Aalto Innovation Services and Aalto Startup Center.
After the project, there is an opportunity to become part of the new deep-tech startup as a co-founder.
The position is full-time and initially funded for 18 months, with the possibility of extension. The salary depends on experience, with a starting salary of 4229,48–4649,25 € per month (gross). The position includes occupational healthcare.
We value a healthy work–life balance and actively support personal and professional development. You will have opportunities to collaborate with leading academic and industry partners and contribute to the development of cutting-edge thin-film metrology instrumentation for the semiconductor industry.
