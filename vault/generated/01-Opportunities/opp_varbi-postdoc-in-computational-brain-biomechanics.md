Job description
Do you have a background in fluid-structure interaction modeling, neuroimaging analysis, soft tissue modelling, or finite element analysis and a passion for advancing the understanding of brain injury? Then this could be a position for you.
In this role, you will take a leading position in advancing brain biomechanics via computational simulation. Your research will focus on:
Develop and validate a finite element model of mouse brain with cerebral vessels and fluid-structure interaction for brain-skull interface
Identify brain injury mechanics by linking computational biomechanics with experimental pathology from early conducted animal tests.
As a postdoctoral fellow, you will work within an interdisciplinary, international, collaborative, and supportive work environment. You will be an integral part of the growing division of Neuronic Engineering with the mission to bridge engineering and medicine to improve the prevention, diagnosis, and treatment of injuries to the human nervous system.
You will work closely with Prof. Svein Kleiven, Assist. Prof. Zhou Zhou, and Assoc. Prof. Xiaogai Li at the Neuronic division, and will also collaborate with Professor Niklas Marklund’s research group at Lund University.
This position will not conduct any new animal experiments. This opportunity is ideal for those seeking to advance their research career in engineering science, computational modelling, and brain health at a leading global institution.
