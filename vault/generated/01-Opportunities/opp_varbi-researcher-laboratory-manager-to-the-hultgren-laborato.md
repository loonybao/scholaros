Job description
The Hultgren Laboratory is a KTH infrastructure open to students and researchers from universities, institutes and industry. It provides a comprehensive suite of instruments for sample preparation, materials characterization and measurement of materials’ properties. While the facility offers measurement services, it is primarily set up for independent use by researchers, who after appropriate training, can operate the tools and instruments themselves. Most research conducted at the laboratory focuses on metallic and hard materials; however, the instrumentation is also well suited to the study of a broad range of other solid-state materials, such as ceramics, polymers, semiconductors, composites and biomaterials.
The overarching goal of the Hultgren Laboratory is to enable high-quality, materials research and education by providing reliable, accessible, and state-of-the-art experimental capabilities. Central to this mission is fostering an efficient, well-supported user environment that promotes both scientific excellence and collaboration across academia and industry.
The Laboratory Manager plays a key role in achieving these objectives. The position is responsible for the overall coordination and day-to-day operation of the laboratory, including managing user access, responding to enquiries, and ensuring an effective onboarding and training process for new users.
The Laboratory Manager will oversee the maintenance, functionality and safe operation of all equipment, while also providing technical support to users in their experimental work. In addition, the role includes responsibility for the laboratory’s financial oversight, including budgeting, operational costs, investments, and income from user fees and services. While primary responsibility for individual instruments is distributed across the team, the Laboratory Manager is expected to take technical leadership for selected equipment aligned with their expertise; for example, XRD, SEM, thermal analysis or mechanical testing.
Beyond operational responsibilities, the Laboratory Manager is expected to contribute strategically to the laboratory’s development by defining a clear vision and initiating plans for long-term improvements in infrastructure, services and instrumentation. The aim is to continuously strengthen the laboratory’s capabilities and enhance its contribution to research excellence and innovation.
