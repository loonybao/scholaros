## Description

Creating quantum networks with rare-earth ions
Job description
Silicon-based microchips are the essential building block of modern electronic hardware, such as computers and cell phones. Can silicon also play a critical role in building the next generation of the internet - the quantum internet? In order to realize a fully functional and scalable quantum network, we are developing highly coherent single photon emitters using erbium ions operating in the telecom C-band and directly embedded in a solid state crystal, such as silicon. Crucially, we are able to realize high-quality light-matter interfaces and directly enhance the photon emission into an optical channel using photonic crystal cavities. By leveraging different isotopes for long-lived and programmable quantum registers, we are planning on realizing all the critical parts for a quantum network using fully integrated and highly scalable structures.
In order to help us achieve our ambitious goals, we are looking for a talented and motivated person, with strong curiousity of rare-earth ion physics, single-emitters in solid-state systems or experimental AMO physics. The successful candidate will work in a dynamic team environment.
