Job description
KTH is seeking a postdoctoral researcher in high-performance data management and artificial intelligence, who will also play a central role in the management of Plasma-PEPSC 2. The project is a European Centre of Excellence in high-performance computing and plasma physics, funded by EuroHPC and coordinated by KTH. It brings together leading European universities, research institutes, and supercomputing centres to advance plasma simulations on Europe's most powerful supercomputers.
The successful candidate will conduct research on scalable methods for managing and analysing large datasets generated by scientific simulations. The work includes efficient data workflows, parallel I/O, data analytics, and the integration of AI methods with the Plasma-PEPSC 2 simulation codes. The research will be carried out in close collaboration with international experts in HPC, AI, and computational plasma physics.
The candidate will also take a central role as project manager of Plasma-PEPSC 2, supporting the scientific and administrative coordination of the project. Responsibilities include planning and monitoring project activities, coordinating international partners, reporting to the funding agency, and organising project meetings and collaborative activities.
