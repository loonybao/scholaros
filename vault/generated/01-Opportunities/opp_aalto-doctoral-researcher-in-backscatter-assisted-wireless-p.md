Aalto University is where science and art meet technology and business. Would you like to develop new wireless positioning methods for challenging non-line-of-sight environments and validate them through real radio experiments?
We are looking for a
Doctoral Researcher in Backscatter-Assisted Wireless Positioning
to join the Department of Information and Communications Engineering at Aalto University.
The position is part of the Research Council of Finland project
Backscatter Assisted Non-line-of-sight Positioning (BANLOS)
. The project investigates how backscatter communications can enable and improve wireless positioning when direct line-of-sight propagation is unavailable or unreliable.
As a doctoral researcher, you will work towards a doctoral degree while contributing to research at the intersection of
wireless positioning, backscatter communications, statistical signal processing, and software-defined radio experimentation
. Depending on your background and interests, your research may emphasize theoretical modelling and algorithms, experimental implementation, or a combination of the two.
Your role and research
Your main goal will be to develop and experimentally validate methods that use backscatter-assisted radio measurements to improve positioning in challenging propagation environments. The detailed research questions will be refined together with your supervisors as your doctoral research progresses.
Your work may include:
Developing signal models, algorithms, and performance analyses for backscatter-assisted non-line-of-sight positioning
Applying detection and estimation theory to questions such as detectability, identifiability, positioning accuracy, and robustness
Designing and implementing software-defined radio prototypes and measurement experiments
Evaluating the proposed methods through simulations, laboratory experiments, and relevant measurement datasets
Publishing your research in high-quality international journals and conferences and presenting your results to the scientific community
Completing doctoral studies and developing the research skills required for a doctoral degree
Participating in teaching and other academic activities of the department to a limited extent
The precise balance between theoretical and experimental research can be tailored according to your background and the scientific direction of the project.
Your research environment and supervision
You will join the BANLOS project in the research group of
Professor Riku Jäntti
at Aalto University's Department of Information and Communications Engineering.
The research environment brings together expertise in wireless communications, radio positioning, statistical signal processing, and experimental radio systems. You will collaborate with researchers and students at Aalto University as well as with the project's wider national and international scientific network.
You will receive regular scientific supervision and support in developing the skills needed to become an independent researcher. As your doctoral work progresses, you will also be encouraged to develop your own research ideas, build scientific networks, and initiate new collaborations.
What we expect from you
We are looking for a motivated, curious, and collaborative candidate who enjoys tackling challenging technical problems and is interested in developing into an independent researcher.
You should have:
A Master's degree, or equivalent, in electrical engineering, communications engineering, signal processing, computer science, applied mathematics, or another closely related field
A strong foundation in wireless communications, statistical signal processing, estimation theory, or a related area
Good mathematical and analytical skills
Programming skills suitable for numerical simulation, signal processing, or experimental research
Good written and spoken English
Motivation and ability to pursue doctoral studies and conduct long-term research
The Master's degree should be completed by the start of the employment.
We particularly value experience or interest in one or more of the following:
Detection and estimation theory
Wireless positioning and localization
Backscatter or wireless communications
Statistical signal processing
Software-defined radio and experimental wireless systems
Python, MATLAB, C/C++, GNU Radio, or comparable research tools
Previous experience in all these areas is not expected. We welcome applicants whose main strengths are either theoretical or experimental, provided that they are motivated to develop the complementary skills needed for the project.
Previous publication experience is not required. In the selection, we will place particular emphasis on the applicant's research potential, academic background, analytical and problem-solving skills, motivation for the research topic, and ability to conduct doctoral research.
Doctoral studies at Aalto University
The position is intended for a researcher pursuing a doctoral degree at the Aalto Doctoral Programme in Electrical Engineering.
The selected candidate must fulfil the eligibility and admission requirements of the Doctoral Programme in Electrical Engineering. If you do not already have a doctoral study right at Aalto University, you will apply for the study right after being selected for the position.
