Job description
The Department of Engineering Mechanics at KTH Royal Institute of Technology is seeking a postdoctoral researcher to develop physics-embedded generative models for turbulent flows. The position is part of the FLOW-AI project, a collaboration between KTH and Linköping University within the SeRC AI4Science initiative. The project is funded by the Swedish e-Science Research Centre (SeRC).
The project aims to develop fast, scalable, and physically consistent generative models for probabilistic prediction of three-dimensional turbulent flows. The research will focus on incorporating physical constraints into diffusion and flow-based generative models, improving inference efficiency, and preserving physical and statistical fidelity. Relevant approaches include diffusion models, flow matching, stochastic interpolants, flow maps, and distillation or other few-step generation methods.
The postdoctoral researcher will develop and evaluate new generative modeling methods using high-fidelity simulation data, with particular emphasis on conservation laws, turbulent statistics, spectral fidelity, and computational efficiency.
