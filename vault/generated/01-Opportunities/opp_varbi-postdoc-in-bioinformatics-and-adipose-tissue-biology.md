Login and apply

Job description
As a postdoctoral researcher, you will carry out bioinformatic and computational analyses of large-scale single-cell and spatial transcriptomics data, together with other omics data, from human adipose tissue, with a focus on tissue cellular composition, metabolic function, and links to obesity, type 2 diabetes and other metabolic diseases. The position includes developing and maintaining bioinformatic analysis pipelines, databases and web-based resources that make clinical and experimental research data accessible to the research community.
You will analyse and interpret complex datasets, present results at research meetings and international conferences, write scientific papers, and contribute to research grant applications. The work is carried out within the Applied Genomics research group at KTH, in close collaboration with clinical and experimental researchers in metabolic disease research, and includes some mentoring of PhD and Master's students.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree. This eligibility requirement must be met no later than the time the employment decision is made.
A doctoral degree in bioinformatics, computational biology, systems biology, genetics, molecular biology, medical science, or a related field.
Documented experience in the analysis of single-cell and/or spatial transcriptomics data.
Strong programming skills in R and/or Python, and experience handling large-scale biological datasets in a Linux or HPC environment.
Fluency in spoken and written English as it is required in day to day work.
Research expertise, documented through publications in bioinformatics, computational biology, or adipose tissue/metabolism research.
Awareness of diversity and equal opportunity issues, with specific focus on gender equality.
Strong collaborative skills and the ability to communicate and work effectively with researchers from different scientific backgrounds.
Ability to work independently and drive your own research forward.

Experience in adipose tissue biology, metabolism, or other metabolic disease research.
Experience developing bioinformatic tools, databases, or web-based resources that make research data accessible.
Experience with multi-omics data integration (e.g., transcriptomics, proteomics, metabolomics).
Experience collaborating within international research consortia.
First-author publications in well-recognized scientific journals.
Teaching ability, e.g. experience of supervision or teaching.
