Project description
Third-cycle subject:
Computer Science
The goal of this project is to develop video-based methods to monitor a human’s physiological parameters such as heart rate, breathing pattern, skin temperature, pupil dilation, etc. Physiological responses can be used to infer underlying states such as concentration, anxiety, and depression, and are more difficult to control for the human than explicit behaviors (e.g. facial expressions and speech). Working systems in clinical and cognitive-scientific practice typically require intrusive devices, so our video-based solution would be both scientifically novel and practically useful. The project will be performed in collaboration with perceptual neuroscience researchers at Karolinska Institutet in Solna, Sweden
