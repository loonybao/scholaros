## Description

What does it take to link data platforms across sectors? Develop a design theory for connecting healthcare and agri-food ecosystems, aligning governance, incentives and business models
Job description
Personalized healthcare interventions require data. Not only from healthcare, but also other sectors like nutrition and food production. Connecting data platforms enables value-creation, such as personalized nutrition advice. But currently, standards, governance models, incentives and business models are not interoperable.
We are looking for a postdoctoral researcher to develop a design theory to overcome the barriers to linking data platforms across the healthcare and agri-food domains.
This one-year position is part of the Redesign project:
https://www.4tu.nl/redesign/
. The TU Delft team focuses on healthcare platforms, and you will collaborate specifically with Wageningen University (on economic modelling of the value of linked data) and University of Twente (on cross-industry data space solutions). You will join Redesign in its final year as a full member of the team, building on the insights generated so far and bringing them together.
Your role
You will:
Develop a design theory that enables the linking of data platforms between healthcare and agri-food.
Integrate two perspectives: the added value of personalised healthcare, and the barriers that data platforms face in facilitating it.
Synthesise the findings of the Redesign project, including the economic modelling and platform design work of our partners, into a coherent theoretical contribution.
Use the design theory as the basis for a research agenda on cross-sector data platforms and ecosystems.
Publish in leading international journals and present at conferences.
Contribute to project meetings, workshops and exchanges with partners and stakeholders in healthcare and agri-food.
Our group
You will work with Prof dr ir Mark de Reuver, and collaborate with others in the group on digital platforms and decentralized data ecosystems. The group takes a sociotechnical approach, with foci on business models, participatory design, ecosystem governance and architectures. Application areas of the broader group include digital healthcare, data spaces, digital product passports and digital identity.
Job requirements
Who you are
You hold a PhD in information systems, digital innovation, technology management, systems engineering, or a closely related field.
You have demonstrable expertise in digital platforms, data ecosystems or data governance, and ideally in design science or theory building.
You are able to integrate insights from different disciplines and methods (e.g. empirical case studies, modelling, design research).
Affinity with healthcare and/or agri-food domains is a plus.
You have a strong publication record or clear potential for high-quality publications.
You work well in a multi-partner, interdisciplinary team and can deliver independently within a short timeframe.
You have excellent written and spoken English.
