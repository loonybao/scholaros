Researcher in simulations of heat-transfer
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Engineering Sciences in Chemistry, Biotechnology and Health
Job description
The position is located at the division of process technology at KTH and will aim at contributing with simulation of sustainable processes for industry. The present post-doctoral project focuses on CFD simulations of heat-transfer. We consider the flows, turbulence, mixing, and heat-transfer in the simulations. We use high-fidelity simulations to capture the details of the chemical reaction inside complex flows. The work consists of simulation using super-computers, code modification/implementation of new models, data analysis and presentation of the result. The data analysis might include some coding in python for creating/modifying short scripts for using Machine -Learning or modal analysis techniques.

Documented experience (peer reviewed articles in top scientific journals) of CFD simulations with COMSOL with a focus on heat-transfer simulations.
