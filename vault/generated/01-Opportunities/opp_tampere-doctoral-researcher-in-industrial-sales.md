Tampere University and Tampere University of Applied Sciences create a unique environment for multidisciplinary, inspirational, and high-impact research and education. Our university community has its competitive strengths in technology, health, and society.

We are inviting applications for a two-year fixed-term
Doctoral Researcher
(PhD student) position in Industrial Sales, with the intention to extend the position for an additional two years. The doctoral research will be positioned in the intersection of three increasingly relevant areas of research: industrial sales and environmental sustainability, with digitalization as an important enabling force for sustainable transformation.
The doctoral researcher will join the Industrial Sales research group
within
the Industrial Engineering and Management Unit

the Center of Industrial Sales Excellence
.
B2B firms face increasing pressure to respond to climate change, resource scarcity, circular economy transitions, decarbonization, and evolving environmental regulation. These developments are reshaping industrial operations and practices and market preferences, and consequently how firms create and capture value and consequent, and thus also customer needs and expectations, drastically changing what is industrial sales and business now and in future. Digital technologies, including artificial intelligence (AI), data analytics, digital platforms, digital product passports, and IoT-based solutions, can enable industrial firms to respond to these challenges. They can support firms in understanding customer needs, demonstrating economic and environmental value, developing circular business and sales models, and facilitating collaboration across value chains and ecosystems.
The selected doctoral candidate will explore how industrial sales can respond to these megatrends: how B2B sales should be managed and how it can contribute to environmental sustainability, and how digital technologies can enable this transformation. Relevant research questions may address, for example, how industrial firms commercialize sustainable and circular and low-carbon offerings and solutions, how sales organizations communicate and demonstrate economic and environmental value, how sustainability changes customer needs and buying behavior within industrial value chains and at the market, and how digital technologies enable new sales approaches, processes, and capabilities. The specific dissertation topic will be developed together with the selected doctoral researcher.

About the research environment
The Center of Industrial Sales Excellence provides a research environment in which academic research is conducted in close collaboration with industrial companies. The doctoral researcher will have opportunities to conduct empirical research with industrial firms, participate in international research collaboration, present research at international conferences, and develop a strong international academic network.
Your supervisors’ research focuses on the transformation of industrial sales and sales management. We examine how environmental sustainability, changing customer buying behavior, and technological development reshape industrial sales at individual, organizational, and ecosystem levels. Particular attention is given to how digital technologies, including AI, data, and digital platforms, can enable new forms of value creation and support the sustainable transformation of industrial business.
