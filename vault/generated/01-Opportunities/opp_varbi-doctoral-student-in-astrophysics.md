Project description
Third-cycle subject: Physics
The PhD student will conduct research in the field of X-ray astronomy, with an emphasis on emission associated with supernovae and their remnants. The project will involve the analysis of observational data, including data mining and data-intensive methods, to address scientific questions such as the properties of compact objects formed in supernovae. The student will work with observations from major X-ray telescopes (e.g., XMM-Newton and Chandra), complemented by multiwavelength data from other facilities.
The student will join the research group of Professor Josefin Larsson, providing opportunities for collaboration on related projects, such as studies of supernova remnants at infrared and optical wavelengths.
Astrophysics research at KTH also includes the development of instrumentation for X-ray astronomy and studies of gamma-ray bursts (
Particle Physics, Astrophysics and Medical Imaging | KTH)
. These activities are part of the Oskar Klein Centre for Cosmoparticle Physics (OKC) at the AlbaNova University Centre (
https://www.okc.albanova.se
), which provides a vibrant international research environment, including a large community of researchers working on supernovae. The centre hosts a broad range of seminars, activities, and opportunities for scientific collaboration.

Only those admitted to postgraduate education may be employed as a doctoral student.
