Job description
Future networked systems increasingly integrate sensing, communication, and computation across large numbers of connected devices. Applications such as industrial automation, cyber-physical systems, and defence-related infrastructure require distributed AI systems that can operate reliably despite limited resources, uncertain communication, and potential adversarial conditions. Our research group studies various analytical and system-level aspects of such systems, with particular emphasis on edge computing, AI, and emerging 6G networks. The work in our group is always conducted in a collaborative environment within the division as well as with external academic groups and industrial partners.
One focus of the group is related to KPI prediction for mission-critical and low latency apliccations. Different KPIs such as coverage, throughput and latency typically evolve due to stochastic effects, however, data-driven prediction methods are becoming more and more relevant for future 6G communication networks. In this context we are seeking for a researcher candidate with experience in this field, that can advance our agenda further for instance through causal AI, extreme-value theory methods or temporal ML architectures and inference methods. The position involves collaborations within our research group as well as with other research groups from different disciplines and universities. It will also comprise collaborations with industrial partners
