## Description

At Aalto University, curiosity moves across disciplines, roles and communities. We bring people together to ask new questions, challenge familiar thinking and turn ideas into impact.
Our work is rooted in responsibility, courage and collaboration, and in a shared commitment to shaping a more sustainable future.
The Department of Economics at Aalto University School of Business is the leading research-oriented economics unit in Finland and focuses on theoretical and empirical microeconomics broadly defined. For more information about the Department, please visit:
http://economics.aalto.fi/en/
The Department of Economics at Aalto University invites applications for a
3-year postdoctoral researcher position in biodiversity economics.
The start date is preferably in fall 2027.
The position is funded by Jane and Aatos Erkko Foundation project titled "From Finnish Biodiversity to Global Insights: Building the Future of Data-Based Natural Capital Assessment (ECO2)". The project brings together economists and ecologists to investigate the interactions between economic activity, biodiversity, and environmental policy. Potential research topics include the economic drivers and consequences of biodiversity change, land use, conservation policies, and economic incentives for biodiversity protection.
What you bring
We welcome applications from candidates with a strong background in empirical environmental economics and an interest in conducting high-quality economic research on biodiversity-related questions. Previous experience in biodiversity research is an advantage but is not required.
Applicants must hold a doctoral degree in economics or a related field or be near completion of the doctorate. The working language is English. Finnish language is not required.
What we offer
The successful candidate will have the opportunity to develop research questions that build on their own expertise and interests, while contributing to the broader objectives of the project. We support your professional growth with varied training and development opportunities shaped around your interests and aspirations.
At the start of the postdoctoral career stage, the gross monthly salary is approximately 4 600 EUR/month. Aalto University provides its employees with occupational health care services and retirement benefits.
We value work-life balance and well-being in all aspects of life. The primary workplace is the Otaniemi campus in Espoo, next to Helsinki. Please note that Aalto expects employees to reside and work in Finland. Life on the revitalised campus is vibrant, featuring stunning architecture, tranquil nature, and a variety of cafes, restaurants and services, with excellent connections via the metro and the new fast tram line.
