## Description

Help build the model library at the heart of UTOPYS, a new national facility for real-time power system simulations.
Job description
Future power systems are converter-dominated, fast, and interconnected across scales that today's modelling tools cannot capture. UTOPYS (Understanding Large and Complex Power Systems) addresses this with a state-of-the-art real-time power system simulator: a large RTDS cluster at TU Delft's ESP Lab that simulates thousands of nodes in full electromagnetic transient (EMT) detail, in real time, and that can be coupled to hardware, SCADA systems and other simulators. UTOPYS is a ten-year national research infrastructure (2026-2036) built by a consortium of eight Dutch universities and research institutes, coordinated by TU Delft. A simulator of this size is only as valuable as the models that run on it. The first phase of UTOPYS is therefore dedicated to the UTOPYS Library: an open, modular and validated collection of EMT component models, reference system models, scenarios and datasets that will serve the consortium, external academic users and industry for the coming decade. TU Delft is recruiting two postdocs to lay its foundations. This vacancy focuses on the library's transmission backbone and on coordinating model integration.
What you will do
Design and build a scalable RSCAD/RTDS model of a large 380/220 kV transmission system: the backbone into which all UTOPYS models are integrated and tested.
Define the library's architecture, interfaces, modelling standards and testing workflow with the partner postdocs.
Act as technical lead for integration: plan integration sprints, review and merge partner contributions, and track what runs, what breaks and why.
Build the library to last: model conversion from other tools, scalability and model reduction, documentation, version control and validation criteria.
Publish your models, datasets and methods open access, and present them in journals and at conferences.
How this position fits
You own the backbone and the integration workflow, while the other TU Delft postdoc develops detailed high-voltage component, substation and protection models that plug into it. Part of your role is technical coordination: agreeing interfaces and standards with partners, preparing the technical side of consortium meetings and demo days, and making sure contributions come together as one working system.
Where and with whom
You will join the Intelligent Electrical Power Grids (IEPG) section and work alongside the UTOPYS postdocs at the partner institutes and with TU Delft staff and PhD students working on HVDC, protection, wide-area stability and cyber-physical power systems. Progress is shared in regular consortium meetings and demonstration days.
What the position offers you
Real-time EMT expertise on a one-of-a-kind infrastructure: a scarce and sought-after skill set in academia, at system operators and in industry.
Visible, citable output. Library models and datasets are published open access with a DOI, credited to their developers, and will be used internationally for years.
Opportunities to contribute to the UTOPYS summer school and RTDS training courses, and represent the programme internationally.
About the department
The research in the Department of Electrical Sustainable Energy is inspired by the technical, scientific, and societal challenges originating from the transition towards a more sustainable society and focuses on four areas:
DC Systems, Energy Conversion and Storage (DCE&S)
Photovoltaic Materials and Devices (PVMD)
Intelligent Electrical Power Grids (IEPG)
High Voltage Technologies (HVT)
The department owns a large ESP Laboratory assembling High Voltage testing, DC Grids testing environment, and large RTDS that is actively used for real-time simulation of future electrical power systems, AC and DC protection and wide-area monitoring and protection. The Intelligent Electrical Power Grid (IEPG) group works on the future of our power system.
