Project description
Third-cycle subject:
physics
The project will focus on theoretical condensed matter physics, with particular emphasis on quantum many-body physics. The research will mainly employ analytical approaches, and particularly field-theoretical methods, to study interacting quantum systems both in and out of equilibrium. Numerical approaches will be used to complement the analytical work. The group’s current research interests include quantum chaos and thermalization, disordered systems, nonequilibrium quantum dynamics, and superconductivity.
