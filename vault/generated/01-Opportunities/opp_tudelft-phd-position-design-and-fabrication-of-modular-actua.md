## Description

Design and fabrication of modular actuator-sensor units for over-actuation
Job description
As chip demand grows and traditional scaling slows down, manufacturers need faster, more precise machines. However, unwanted vibrations in high-speed systems limit how quickly and accurately chips can be made. Current solutions can’t handle these complex vibrations. ETCH develops a new approach called over-actuation: placing many tiny sensors and actuators inside machine parts to control vibrations exactly where they happen. This PhD position will be developing methods for design and fabrication of modular actuator–sensor units, including shaping of the transducers, interfacing to the target system, embedding of the drive and sense solutions and connections into communication topologies for control.
Job requirements
A suitable candidate is strongly motivated about the research content and has a suitable preparation to conduct research that consists of both theoretical and experimental tasks:
1. Strong interest and curiosity in:
a) Piezoelectric actuators and sensors;
b) Precision manufacturing processes;
c) Mechanical interfacing of structures;
d) System design and embedded circuits;
2. MSc (or equivalent) degree in an *engineering* or *natural sciences* discipline:
Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics, Electrical Engineering, Materials Science, Applied Physics, or similar;
3. Experience in *one* (or more) of the following fields:
a) Piezoelectric actuators and sensors: design, manufacturing and experimental evaluation of piezoelectric-enabled sensing and/or actuation systems;
b) Precision manufacturing methods: micromachining methods, subractive manufacturing methods, multi-material additive manufacturing, or similar;
c) Physics of materials adhesion and interfacing: adhesion of dissimilar materials and composites, surface physics, and similar;
d) Design and implementation of electronic circuits: power amplifiers, sense amplifiers, embedded systems and similar;
4. Beneficial experiences that will give an advantage (the more the better):
a) Additive and subtractive manufacturing methods;
b) Design and building of mechatronic systems and experimental set-ups;
c) Control engineering and dynamics analysis methods;
d) Numeric modelling methods application on piezoelectrics, funtional materials compliant mechanisms, composite materials, or similar;
e) Publication history in (international) conferences or journals;
5. Excellent level of spoken and written English.
