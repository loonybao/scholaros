The research is conducted in the
Regulatory Genomics group
at the Biomedicine unit, MET. We investigate the spatial and temporal dynamics of gene regulation in disease. Using single-cell and spatial genomics, our group maps cell type-specific regulatory programs relevant to disease onset and progression, with the overarching goal of understanding how these regulatory processes reside within the disease microenvironment, and how genetic variation influences them.
The Faculty of Medicine and Health Technology (MET)
is dedicated to pursuing world-class research ranging from clinical medicine and translational medicine, through molecular biology, biotechnology and cell biology, to biomedical engineering, biomicrosystems, biomaterials, and biorobotics. We conduct internationally acclaimed basic and applied research.
Job description
We are looking for a talented and motivated postdoctoral researcher to join the Regulatory Genomics research group in investigating how microglial states emerge in response to neurodegenerative disease-related stressors and how these states manifest within diseased brain tissue. The project will employ advanced single-cell and spatial genomic techniques developed by the group as well computational inference approaches to study glial gene regulation.

We are seeking a versatile and independent researcher with experience supporting experimental or computational aspects of neuroscience research and/or single-cell or spatial genomics.
