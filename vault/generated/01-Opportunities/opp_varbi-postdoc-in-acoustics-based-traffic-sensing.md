Job description
Join KTH's research in engineering acoustics at the Marcus Wallenberg Laboratory (MWL) and develop the next generation of acoustics-based traffic sensing. Cost-effective roadside microphones, combined with signal processing and machine learning, can provide continuous, real-time information on traffic flows, vehicle types and traffic noise, whereas cities today rely on short and intrusive measurement campaigns.
The position is part of two projects funded by
Digital Futures
, carried out together with the City of Stockholm, Nacka municipality and other municipalities in the region: a societal innovation project expanding an acoustic sensor network across the Stockholm region, and a proof-of-concept project developing a validated roadside monitoring system. You will build on established testbeds and unique datasets from Stockholm's urban environment.
You will deploy acoustic sensor units in urban environments, develop robust methods for detecting and classifying vehicle pass-bys, from processing on the sensor (edge) to the cloud, validate the results against reference measurements, and make data available through APIs and visualisation tools for municipal users. The position also includes scientific publishing, co-supervising students and contributing to new proposals and innovation activities.
