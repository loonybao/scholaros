## Description

Research assistant in decision and control systems
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
Job description
We are now seeking a research assistant for a paid, fixed-term position lasting 6 months at KTH’s Department of Decision and Control Systems. We welcome applications from people in the beginning of their career who wish to contribute to research projects, for example through laboratory work, compilation of experimental data, and other related tasks.
Qualifications
Requirements
Post-secondary education in software development, information systems, or computer engineering, for example from a college, university, or higher vocational education.
Experience with React, TypeScript, C#, ASP.NET Core, Entity Framework Core, Python, SQL (PostgreSQL), Docker, Docker Compose and Git.
You must be fluent in English both in writing and orally as it is required in the daily work.
Ability to work independently and have no problem collaborating with others.
Preferred qualifications
Experience with the traffic simulator SUMO (e.g. netconvert, randomTrips, FCD output).
Experience with geospatial data and tools (geopandas, Shapely, QGIS, OpenStreetMap, coordinate reference systems).
Basic knowledge of wireless communication and base stations (path loss, SINR, data rate requirements).
Experience with WebSockets, REST APIs, and asynchronous programming.
Experience with Swedish open data APIs (Trafikverket, Stockholm stad) and ability to read Swedish.
Pedagogic skills
Good ability to express yourself orally and in writing
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
