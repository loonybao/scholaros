Job description
We are looking for postdoctoral fellows to contribute to several projects related to thermohydraulics and safety of small modular reactors (SMRs). The work will include analysis in support of design and development of large-scale experimental facilities to be constructed and operated at KTH and Oskarshamn. Different facilities will be designed to address important thermohydraulic phenomena relevant to both water and liquid lead cooled SMRs. The work will be a part of national projects supported by Swedish Energy Agency and has strong links with collaborative projects in Europe.
