Project description
Third-cycle subject: Geodesy and Geoinformatics, with specialisation in Geoinformatics
We are seeking a doctoral student for a two-year licentiate position in
DISSECT
, which develops data-driven models and decision support for emission-free, resource-efficient and electrified construction.
The doctoral student will work with DISSECT’s material and logistics layers. The research may include GeoBIM and 3D voxel models of ground conditions, quantities and material flows; material tracking; volume and mass estimation; inverse geological modelling; and analysis of sensor and telematics data from machines and transports. The logistics part may include optimization of construction processes, mass transports, circular material flows and charging constraints.
Methodologically, the work may combine geospatial data management, database technology, data science, machine learning, simulation and optimization, including model predictive control and reinforcement learning. The aim is digital-twin-based decision support for electrified groundwork construction.
The position is placed at the Division of Geoinformatics, KTH, and linked to ITRL and the DISSECT consortium. The initial goal is a licentiate degree after two years, but the research and doctoral studies are planned to continue toward a doctoral degree, subject to continued funding and formal decisions.
