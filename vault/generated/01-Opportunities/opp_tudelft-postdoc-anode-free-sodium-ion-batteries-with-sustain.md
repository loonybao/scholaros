## Description

Job description
Within this Postdoc project, the successful candidate will be involved in the development and investigation of next-generation sodium-ion batteries through the following key activities:
Development of anode-free sodium-ion battery systems.
Deposition of artificial interphases using vacuum-based thin-film deposition techniques.
Post-treatment and modification of electrodes through wet-chemical and solvent-free processing routes.
Physicochemical characterization of synthesized and processed materials.
Assembly of battery cells and electrochemical evaluation of electrode performance.
Mechanistic studies and postmortem analyses of electrode materials, including the use of in situ characterization techniques.
