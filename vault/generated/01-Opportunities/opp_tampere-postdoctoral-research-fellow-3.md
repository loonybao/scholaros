We are looking for a
Postdoctoral Research Fellow
to work on deep learning methodologies for machine and robot perception.
The position is funded by the Horizon Europe project OPERA (Open Perception, Learning, and Decision-Making Toolbox for General Purpose Robot AI), and the selected applicant will be a member of the Signal Analysis and Machine Intelligence group led by Professor
Moncef Gabbouj
, and closely collaborate with the Machine Intelligence group led by Professor
Alexandros Iosifidis
, on achieving the goals and objectives of Tampere University team within the project.
The position is full-time starting from November 1
st

Unit of Computing Sciences
within Tampere University's
Faculty of Information Technology and Communication Sciences
. The workplace is located at Hervanta Campus of Tampere University, Finland.
JOB DESCRIPTION
The postdoctoral researcher fellow at Tampere University will propose and develop novel deep learning methodologies, disseminate their results in high-quality publications in international conferences and journals, interact with project partners in project activities, integrate their methodologies and related baselines to the toolbox of the project. The selected postdoctoral researcher fellow will also be responsible for the project deliverables related to the tasks below, arranging and attending period meetings with the researchers in the team and in the consortium, publishing high-quality papers in relevant conferences and journals. The research topics for the selected applicant are the following:
Efficient human-centric active perception and learning
Social signal analysis and collaborative learning
Learning for increased robustness to variations and adaptation
Active sensor fusion for increased robustness and adaptivity
In addition, the duties of the postdoctoral researcher fellow may include some teaching tasks (5-10 %) in topics connected to their expertise.
REQUIREMENTS
A completed doctoral degree in computer science, computer engineering, or another related field
Proven expertise in Machine Learning theory and methods, and related Deep Learning approaches
Excellent knowledge of programming in Python and scientific libraries used for Deep Learning (PyTorch or TensorFlow). Ability to work using multiple Deep Learning libraries
Ability to pursue independent scholarly activity and lead systematic scientific research related to the design of Deep Learning methods and models
A curious mind and solid skills in critical thinking and problem solving
Very good communication skills in English, and ability to lead and collaborate in multidisciplinary teams
Ability to write research papers in high-quality peer-reviewed journals or conferences in the field of Machine Learning and Computer Vision
