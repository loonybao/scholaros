Project description
Third-cycle subject:
Engineering Mechanics
Within EU doctoral network F
airCFD
, this project will combine high-performance computing, experiments and artificial intelligence in non-Newtonian flows, with the aim of improving rheological characterisation and modelling. In particular, we will design a digital twin rheometer to reliably measure complex fluid parameters in time-varying and chaotic flows.
We will use modern AI techniques to exploit spatial patterns in the computational and experimental data and optimize parameter sensitivity. The researcher will join the dynamic Complex
Fluids Research group
, have access to the supercomputing facilities at KTH, the Swedish e-Science Research Centre (
SeRC

FLOW
