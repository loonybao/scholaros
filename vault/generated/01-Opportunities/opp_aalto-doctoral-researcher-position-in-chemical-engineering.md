Your role and goals
Trickle bed reactors are in a central position in many refinery operations, both in traditional oil refining as well as treatment of novel bio-based feedstocks. Trickle bed operations have been studied from many angles, including fluid flow models, chemical kinetics, and mass transfer. Despite these, there is still a need for dynamic models of trickle bed reactors with both industrial and scientific importance. Your task would be to develop such models based on literature, in collaboration with an industrial partner. You should:
Collect and critically analyze relevant literature
Build and analyze models for predicting dynamic trickle bed reactor operations, including material and energy balances, reaction kinetics, mass and heat transfer, phase equilibria, fluid flow, and process control and operation
Collaborate with other members of the research group and external partners to achieve project goals
Present research findings at conferences and publish results in peer-reviewed journals
