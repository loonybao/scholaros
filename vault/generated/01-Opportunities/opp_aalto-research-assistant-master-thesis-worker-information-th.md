The Information Theory research team at Aalto University (Department of Information and Communications Engineering, School of Electrical Engineering) invites applications for a 6-month
Research Assistant position for master thesis worker
.
About the team
We work across discrete mathematics and information theory, including
coding theory, combinatorial algorithms, design theory, and graph theory.
Computational methods are central; the team operates a 500‑core cluster.
We both compete with and leverage AI; see:
https://www.aalto.fi/en/news/researcher-cracks-new-kissing-number-bounds-besting-ai-in-the-process
Project focus
One class of structures we study is Hadamard matrices — orthogonal combinatorial objects with applications in quantum technology. In this position, you will develop and implement computational methods to investigate how a technique called switching transforms various types of Hadamard matrices.
Qualifications
Background in mathematics or computer science
Excellent programming skills and experience with C
Interest in computational discrete mathematics and algorithmic experimentation
NOTE!
This position is open only for Aalto University students.
What we offer
Paid employment for master's thesis work (salary: 2622 €/month).
The position is fixed term from 1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027.
