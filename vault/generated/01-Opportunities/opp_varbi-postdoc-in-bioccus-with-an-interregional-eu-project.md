Job description
The use of biomass as an energy material and industrial feedstock is well developed in Sweden. This puts the country in an excellent position for the deployment of negative emission technologies based on capture, utilization and storage of bioCO2. The current position is part of an Interregional EU project within the Baltic region (Interreg Baltic) that aims at exploring and developing value chains for bioCO2, with the ultimate aim to replace fossil carbon with bio-carbon in certain industrial sectors. KTH will work closely with different biogas producer in the Baltic region and design and develop processes for separation and upgrading of bioCO2. The work includes field measurements at the biogas plant, lab experiments to estimate unknown model parameters, and theoretical in-silico process design calculations and cost-estimations. As a post-doc researcher in this project, you will be working on the junction between academic research and industrial development, interact with partners and stakeholders from various fields, and develop hands-on experience on industrial deployment of sustainable process technologies.
