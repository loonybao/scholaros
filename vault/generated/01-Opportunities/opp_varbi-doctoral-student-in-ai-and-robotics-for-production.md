Project description
Third-cycle subject: Production engineering
We are seeking a motivated PhD student in intelligent robotics for production, with a focus on robot learning and AI-enabled automation. The research aims to develop intelligent robotic systems that combine learning-based methods and advanced algorithms to support flexible, adaptive and human-centred industrial production. The work includes reinforcement learning, foundation models, world models and dexterous manipulation with applications in real industrial environments.
The PhD student will collaborate closely with leading researchers, industrial partners, and international collaborators, contributing to both scientific advances and industrial innovation. The position is part of the Center of Excellence for Production Research (XPRES) and includes collaboration with leading European universities. The doctoral student will also join the new Production Systems and Automation unit at KTH, an inclusive and internationally oriented environment for research in intelligent robotics and automation for future production systems. We welcome applicants with diverse backgrounds and perspectives.

Only those admitted to postgraduate education may be employed as a doctoral student.
