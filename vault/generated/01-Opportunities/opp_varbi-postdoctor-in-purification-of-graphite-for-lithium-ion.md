Job description
At the department of chemical engineering, research is conducted with the common objective to develop sustainable processes. We are looking for a postdoctoral researcher to work in a project focusing on purification of natural graphite for use in batteries. The work is part of a collaboration project with the objective to develop both thermal and chemical processes for this purpose. At KTH the work is focused on developing sustainable chemical processes. The work includes design and experimental studies of simple model systems as well as applied studies in collaboration with the other project partners. The applicant should have extensive experience in laboratory work and is expected to be able to work both independently and in collaboration with colleagues at KTH and other organizations.
The project is led by RISE and is a collaboration between RISE, KTH and Uppsala University, funded by the Swedish Energy Agency. This is a full-time position for two years. The start date is open for discussion.
