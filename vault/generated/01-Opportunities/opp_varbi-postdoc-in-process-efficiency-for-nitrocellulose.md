Job description
Would you like to make nitrocellulose production more resource-efficient and sustainable? We are looking for a postdoc at the competence centre Svenskt Krut at KTH, where academia, government agencies and industry jointly strengthen Sweden's capability in nitrocellulose (NC).
I projektet analyserar vi nitreringsprocessen och delprocesserna för att återvinna och återanvända syror, tvättvatten och lösningsmedel
In the project we analyse the nitration process and sub-processes for rthe ecovery and reuse of acids, washing water and solvents. Your role is to map and quantify process and residual streams, establish mass balances and evaluate recovery techniques such as distillation, membrane processes and sorption.
You will carry out laboratory and smaller pilot trials on the most promising solutions, perform a techno-economic analysis and propose a greener NC process. Flow simulation and AI-supported data analysis are central tools. The work is carried out at the Department of Fibre and Polymer Technology and in collaboration with FOI and industrial partners.
