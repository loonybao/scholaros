## Description

Researcher in systems biology of photosynthesis
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Engineering Sciences in Chemistry, Biotechnology and Health
Job description
The researcher will also be responsible for design and execution of systems biology experiments involving cyanobacteria and plant systems. This entails development and application of interaction proteomics methods, maintenance and optimization of mass-spectrometry equipment, analysis of proteomics data. The researcher will also be responsible for training other lab members in liquid chromatography-mass spectrometry
