Job description
This project investigates energy optimisation and resilience of critical infrastructure, focusing on hospitals. The researcher will model hospital HVAC, heating, cooling and backup energy systems and examine their dependencies on wider energy and healthcare supply chains. Research combines dynamic energy simulation, scenario-based risk and resilience assessment, life-cycle assessment (LCA) and environmental product declaration (EPD) data to quantify energy performance, environmental impacts and service continuity during climate-related disruptions. Building on these analyses, the researcher will develop and assess advanced control and decision-support methods, potentially including AI, to improve efficiency, flexibility and operational resilience. Validation will emphasise realistic operating constraints and collaboration with healthcare and infrastructure stakeholders. The position includes 20% teaching or departmental duties.
