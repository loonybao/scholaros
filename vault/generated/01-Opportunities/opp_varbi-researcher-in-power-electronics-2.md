Job description
The energy system is undergoing a paradigm shift with the rapid integration of renewable energy, electric vehicles and large-scale AI data centers, which are connected to the grid through power electronic converters. Power electronic converters play a pivotal role in enabling this transition, while also introducing new challenges related to system dynamics, stability, and control.
This project focuses on the development of modeling, stability analysis, control, and hardware solutions for converter-dominated power systems. Application areas include power electronic systems supporting AI data centers. The project is funded by EU Clean Energy Transition Partnership and Swedish Energy Agency, in collaboration with Hitachi Energy and grid operators.
