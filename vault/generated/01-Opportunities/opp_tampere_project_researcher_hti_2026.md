## Description

Project Researcher positions in the Pervasive Interaction Research Group
(TAUCHI Research Center), associated with the Tampere Accessibility Unit.
Research areas: Human-Technology Interaction, XR, accessibility, multimodal
interaction, inclusive digital technology for work and society. Potential
project assignments include: human-centred simulation and training in
networked defence environments; human-AI collaboration in industrial
settings; human-centred AI for meaningful and efficient work; conversational
and accessible interaction with industrial digital twins; novel interactive
applications for inclusive work environments; human augmentation in
industrial and defence contexts. Work may involve software development,
VR/AR/XR, experimental design, user evaluations, and collection/analysis of
objective and subjective data. Some selected candidates are expected to
pursue doctoral studies; after obtaining doctoral study rights the title can
change to Doctoral Researcher.

Application materials: motivation letter (max 1 page, independently
written); CV including a 1-2 minute video self-introduction link; a clear
list of relevant skills; portfolio of software, research outputs or design
projects; degree certificates; transcript of completed Master's degree.

Note: some projects may be subject to legal, sanctions or export-control
restrictions affecting candidate eligibility.
