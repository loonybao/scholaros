## Description

Conduct advanced research into atmospheric chemistry and air quality by deploying innovative measurement techniques for the atmosphere, including low-cost air quality sensors and miniature instruments.
Job description
Air pollution remains one of the most pressing environmental and public health challenges worldwide. To understand the sources, transformation and transport of atmospheric pollutants, a combination of advanced observations, innovative instrumentation and data analysis is required. Recent developments in miniature atmospheric instruments, low-cost air quality monitoring technologies and drone-based measurement platforms are creating new opportunities to study the composition of the atmosphere at unprecedented spatial, temporal and vertical scales.
In this position, you will contribute to research into atmospheric chemistry, aerosol science and air quality monitoring. You will use atmospheric instruments and low-cost air quality sensors to monitor particulate matter and gaseous pollutants in urban environments.
You will play a leading role in designing and conducting field measurement campaigns, including upcoming drone-based air quality observation campaigns. Your responsibilities will include operating atmospheric instrumentation, ensuring data quality and analyzing large observational datasets. This role involves combining practical, hands-on instrumentation with data-driven scientific analysis to improve our understanding of atmospheric processes and air pollution dynamics.
A key aspect of the role involves disseminating research through high-quality, peer-reviewed publications. You will be expected to contribute to scientific writing, supervise junior researchers where appropriate and present research findings at national and international conferences.
You will join a collaborative, internationally recognized research group specializing in atmospheric science. This position offers an excellent opportunity to contribute to innovative research and participate in collaborations with academic partners.
Job requirements
We are looking for a highly motivated researcher with a strong background in atmospheric measurements and data analysis. You will have:
A PhD in atmospheric science, atmospheric chemistry, environmental science, aerosol science, physics, environmental engineering or a closely related discipline.
Demonstrated expertise in atmospheric chemistry, aerosol science or air quality research;
Experience with atmospheric instrumentation, including miniature atmospheric instruments and/or low-cost air quality sensors.
Experience in planning and conducting field measurements and atmospheric monitoring campaigns.
Willingness to participate in intensive fieldwork, including upcoming drone-based air quality measurement campaigns.
Strong skills in analyzing large atmospheric datasets using programming languages such as Python, MATLAB or R.
Experience with atmospheric data processing, quality assurance and statistical analysis.
A strong publication record of peer-reviewed scientific articles.
Excellent scientific writing skills and experience in preparing manuscripts for publication in international journals.
Ability to work independently while contributing effectively within an interdisciplinary research team.
Excellent command of written and spoken English.
Experience with drone-based atmospheric measurements, sensor calibration and validation, or instrument development would be advantageous.
