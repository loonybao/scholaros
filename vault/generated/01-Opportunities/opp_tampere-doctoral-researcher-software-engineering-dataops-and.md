We are inviting applications for a Doctoral Researcher (PhD student) position in the areas of software engineering, DataOps and MLOps. The successful candidate will pursue a doctoral degree at Tampere University.
Job description
The position will be placed within the unit of Computing Sciences in Tampere University's Faculty of Information Technology and Communication Sciences. Software engineering is one of the core areas of education and research of the faculty. Read more about the research group from here:
the TASE - Software Engineering Research Center
.
Our research center develops efficient ways to produce better quality software in a predictable and economically sustainable way. The position belongs to the xOps research group, which studies the operations of AI-driven systems, from the data pipelines of AI systems to the deployment of language models.
You will focus on the research of DataOps practices and maturity levels for AI-intensive systems, as the data foundation for the operations of AI-based models such as Machine Learning and Language based Models.
Your work will include:
Investigating the state of practice of DataOps in AI-intensive systems and identifying which practices are missing.
Defining and validating maturity levels for DataOps practices that ensure data quality, governance, and transferability.
Designing guidelines and a framework that record the quality, provenance, license, and sensitivity of data along the lifecycle of AI-based models.
Evaluating how systematic data management affects the quality of AI-based models, including language models.
As a Doctoral Researcher you are expected to perform overviews of state of the art, to study the principles of DataOps, MLOps, data quality and governance, and empirical software engineering methods. You are also expected to develop pipelines and frameworks, to generate empirical results, to analyze and validate them on use case(s) through comparison with the state of the art.
The results of your research will be disseminated in scientific peer-reviewed journals, international conferences, doctoral network events and events with industry. The tasks of doctoral researchers also include teaching duties that amount to approximately 5 % of their annual working hours, unless there are justified reasons to the contrary.

You need to have a master’s degree in:
Software engineering or Data science (preferred)
Computer science or Machine learning
You also need:
Proficiency in Python or a related programming language.
Experience in creating xOps pipelines (e.g., DevOps, MLOps, DataOps).
Strong spoken and written communication skills in English, including satisfaction of the language skill requirements of the doctoral programme of the discipline.
Beneficial skills (not mandatory):
Experience with data engineering, data versioning, and CI/CD tools (e.g., DVC, MLflow, Delta Lake, GitHub Actions, or equivalent).
Knowledge of data quality, data governance, and related standards, with the ability to apply them to real-world AI-based systems.
Familiarity with empirical software engineering methods (surveys, case studies, design science research) or with language models and their training data.
