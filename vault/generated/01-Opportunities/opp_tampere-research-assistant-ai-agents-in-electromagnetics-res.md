## Description

Tampere University and Tampere University of Applied Sciences create a unique environment for multidisciplinary, inspirational and high-impact research and education. Our universities community has its competitive edges in technology, health and society.
www.tuni.fi/en
The research group of
Electromechanics
at the
Electrical Engineering Unit
at the
Faculty of Information Technology and Communication Sciences
at Tampere University focuses on numerical electromagnetics and magnetic materials. We work at the interface of electromagnetic theory, computational science, physics of magnetic materials and electrical engineering applications of practical importance. Our vision is to integrate advanced electromagnetic modeling tools together with practical design of electromagnetic energy conversion devices, such as electrical machines, transformers, inductors and energy harvesters.
We are part of a
Research Council of Finland Centre of Excellence
on “High-Speed Electromechanical Energy Conversion Systems” which aims at the development of next generation high-speed electrical machines for energy conversion and transport applications. Our part is related to the development of numerical modeling techniques for the high-frequency behavior of electrical machine windings. We are also running a 5-year
European Research Council funded project
“Controllable Magnetic Cores for Transformers and Electrical Machines” related to real-time control of magnetic flux paths in electromagnetic devices. Both projects rely heavily on numerical modeling of magnetic fields in different applications using in-house computational codes.
JOB DESCRIPTION
We are now looking for a Research Assistant to explore means of applying AI agents in our ongoing research. The objective is to identify and evaluate promising applications of AI agents in computational electromagnetics and related software development. The exact topic will be decided based on the background and interests of the selected candidate. The work may involve, for example,
development of AI agents for integration and maintenance of computational codes written in different languages (in particular MATLAB, Python and Fortran)
development of AI agent-based workflows for verification, validation and benchmarking computational electromagnetics software
development of AI agents for automated implementation of computational electromagnetics algorithms based on scientific literature
The topics may also be suitable for B.Sc. or M.Sc. theses.
REQUIREMENTS
A successful candidate has a B.Sc. or M.Sc. study right in a Finnish university, an excellent study record in a relevant engineering field, experience in programming and interest in applying AI tools in electromagnetic modeling. In particular, experience with or interest in some of the following are appreciated:
programming in MATLAB, Python or Fortran
scientific computing, e.g., the finite element method
dynamic electromagnetics, electrical machines and transformers
open-weight AI models, local AI deployment tools (e.g. Ollama, OpenCode) and agentic AI workflows
customizing large language models
They can also demonstrate high-quality writing and reporting skills. The position requires good English language skills.
