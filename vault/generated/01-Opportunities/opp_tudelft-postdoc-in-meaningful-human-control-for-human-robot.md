## Description

Join TU Delft as a postodoc to develop AI-enable robotic systems for responsible human-robot collaboration.
Job description
Autonomous robots working in human-centered environments must do more than react to immediate sensor input. They need to be able to estimate uncertainty, explain their decisions, adapt to human preferences and values, and act safely and reliably in changing conditions. In the EU-funded OPERA project, TU Delft contributes to General-Purpose AI for robotics by developing methods that combine fast System 1-style behavior with more deliberate System 2-style reasoning, adaptation, and decision-making.
In this postdoc position, you will investigate how robots can interact with humans to make transparent, trustworthy, safe and reliable decisions. The research combines AI, robotics, and cognitive and social sciences to develop human-robot collaboration that supports meaningful human control, enabling humans to understand, influence and take responsibility for the system’s behavior.
The developed methods will be evaluated in real-world multi-stakeholder scenarios, integrating data-driven approaches such as inverse reinforcement learning and imitation learning with cognitive and socio-technical models. The research will also contribute to the development of a broader ethical and governance frameworks for ethical human-robot interaction, addressing fairness, bias, privacy, safety, and oversight to support trustworthy and responsible AI-enabled robotic systems.
Job requirements
We are looking for applicants who have or expect to receive a PhD degree before joining the project. This positions welcomes applicants from different technical backgrounds. For example, you have expertise in AI with an interest in developing models that can identify and align to complex social contexts; you have a focus on human-robot interaction addressing methods that empower humans to understand and steer the behavior of these; you have a socio-technical or design background, implementing state-of-the-art AI methods or developing robotics systems towards human-centred applications. If one or more of these areas describe you, we encourage your to apply. We particularly welcome applicants with experience or interest in interdisciplinary research.
