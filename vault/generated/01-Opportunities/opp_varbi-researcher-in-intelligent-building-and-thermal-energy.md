## Description

Researcher in intelligent building and thermal energy systems
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Architecture and Built Environment
Job description
We seek a researcher to develop intelligent heating and cooling systems for buildings and district energy networks using physics-based and data-driven models, advanced control and optimization. The work covers HVAC systems, heat pumps, thermal storage, district heating and cooling, and renewable and recovered energy. Tasks include developing and validating models and digital twins, analyzing measured and simulated data, and assessing energy efficiency, flexibility, emissions, indoor environmental quality and climate resilience, including overheating. The researcher will conduct independent studies, publish results, prepare funding applications and collaborate with academic partners, industry and public authorities. Teaching and supervision may be included.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree
Documented research expertise in building energy systems, thermal energy systems, building services engineering, energy engineering or related fields, demonstrated through scientific publications.
Experience in modeling and simulation of energy systems and analysis of numerical or measured data.
Experience in control, optimization, or data-driven methods relevant to heating and cooling systems.
Ability to plan and conduct research independently and collaborate in interdisciplinary projects.
Very good oral and written communication skills in English, since the position involves scientific publication and international collaboration.
Preferred qualifications
Experience in model predictive control, reinforcement learning, physics-informed optimization or digital twins.
Knowledge of heat pumps, thermal storage, recovered and renewable energy, and district heating and cooling.
Programming in Python, MATLAB, or similar, and use of simulation tools such as IDA ICE, TRNSYS, or EnergyPlus.
Experience validating models or control strategies using measurements from real facilities.
Knowledge of overheating, climate adaptation, thermal comfort and indoor environmental quality.
Demonstrated teaching ability and experience in teaching, supervision or research communication.
Experience in research leadership, funding applications, collaboration and practical uptake of research results.
Knowledge of diversity and equal opportunities, particularly gender equality.
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
