Job description
The European Union (EU), through initiatives such as REPowerEU, REFuelEU, and FuelEU Maritime, is driving the transition toward lower greenhouse gas emissions. Green hydrogen is expected to play a key role, with part of the demand being met through imports. Ammonia is emerging as a promising hydrogen carrier due to its suitability for storage, transport, and applications such as ammonia cracking, shipping, and power generation. As part of the European MetNH3Energy project, you will assess the techno-economic and environmental performance of three ammonia value chains to support the identification of sustainable pathways for carbon footprint reduction. The role involves conducting process modeling, ex-ante techno-economic (TEA) and life cycle (LCA) assessments. Because several technologies under evaluation are not yet commercial, you will apply scenario-based “what-if” analyses to evaluate their future environmental and economic impacts. This work will contribute to developing sustainable ammonia and hydrogen solutions that support the EU's energy transition objectives.
