The Textile Chemistry group is now looking for a
Doctoral Researcher in Textile Chemistry
The Textile Chemistry group (
https://www.aalto.fi/en/department-of-bioproducts-and-biosystems/textile-chemistry
) focuses on environmentally friendly textile coloration and post-treatment, textile recycling, and eTextiles.
We are now looking for a Doctoral Researcher to develop functional and adaptive textiles with tailored infrared (IR), thermal, optical, and electrical properties.
The research will be carried out in the
Department of Bioproducts and Biosystems
in the School of Chemical Engineering at Aalto University, in close collaboration with national and international research partners.
About the research
In this project, colorants will be extracted from materials such as bark and needles and evaluated in cotton dyeing and screen printing. Tannin-rich fractions will be tested as bio-based aids for color fixation. Laccase treatment will be tested as a way to improve color formation or retention. Washing, light exposure, and rubbing tests will show which approaches produce durable color. The research is planned in collaboration with the Natural Resources Institute Finland (Luke). The doctoral research will be done as a part of Aalto University Bioinnovation Center’s Doctoral School.
The research work includes
Extract, characterize, and standardize colorants from selected forest industry residues, accounting for variation between batches.
Design cotton dyeing experiments to assess how extract composition and fixation treatments affect color strength, shade consistency, and retention.
Develop cotton screen-printing pastes and evaluate color yield, print definition, and fixation.
Compare selected laccase treatments with appropriate controls and use chemical analyses to distinguish polymerization from aggregation.
Measure washing, light, and rubbing fastness of dyed and printed cotton and investigate colorant release and degradation.
Publish research findings, present results, and complete doctoral studies and a thesis.
Requirements
A relevant master’s degree, preferably in
biochemistry
or
textile chemistry

A strong academic record and an interest in plant-derived colorants and textile coloration.
Hands-on laboratory experience, demonstrated through a master’s thesis or equivalent research, and the ability to design controlled experiments and interpret quantitative data.
The ability to work independently, collaborate across disciplines, and communicate research clearly in English.
Excellent verbal and written English communication skills
Experience with textile dyeing, printing, or colorfastness testing is particularly relevant for candidates with a textile chemistry background. For candidates with a biochemistry background, experience with enzyme assays, enzyme-catalyzed reactions, or phenolic compounds is especially valuable. Plant extraction, chromatography, spectroscopy, and mass spectrometry are also relevant.
