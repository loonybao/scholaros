Aalto University is where science and art meet technology and business. We shape a sustainable future by making research breakthroughs in and across our disciplines, sparking the game changers of tomorrow and creating novel solutions to major global challenges. Our community is made up of 16 000 students and 5 200 employees, including 446 professors. Our campus is in Espoo, Greater Helsinki, Finland.  Diversity is part of who we are, and we actively work to ensure our community’s diversity and inclusiveness. This is why we warmly encourage qualified candidates from all backgrounds to join our community.
We are now looking for a
Research Assistant
at Aalto University School of Business for a project examining how joint versus sole custody affects children’s and parents’ long-term outcomes, including education, health and employment. The applicant must be enrolled in a bachelor’s or master’s degree programme. We hope the selected candidate can start as soon as possible, or by 11.1.2027 the latest.
Your role
The main role is to help build a unique research dataset by collecting information on custody decisions from Finnish district courts. This will involve, for example:
contacting courts
coordinating access and data collection arrangements
systematically extracting and recording information from court documents.
To succeed in this role, you will need
A proactive, independent approach, with the confidence to take ownership of communication with courts and follow requests through to completion.
Strong organisational skills, attention to detail and sound judgment when handling confidential information.
An interest in empirical research in economics or a related social science.
Strong capability and positive attitude toward data processing and analysis.
Excellent Finnish and English language skills, both written and spoken, is required.
Bachelor’s or Master’s level studies in a relevant field.
What we offer
Opportunity to participate in research
as part of a vibrant international community: You will work within a community of students, researchers, and other professionals advancing societally significant goals in science and education.
Support, coaching and sparring
when you feel you need it.
Great possibilities for
competence development and learning
. This is an excellent opportunity for a student considering a PhD to gain hands-on experience in an ambitious research project and work closely with the research team. Depending on your interests and the project’s progress, the work could also provide a foundation for a master’s thesis or your own future research.
Meaningful and inspiring environment

A culture where everyone is valued.

We aim to combine the best of remote and on-site work and offer flexible working hours. Your on-site workplace is located on the Otaniemi campus, with diverse services and excellent transport connections.
