We invite applications for a
doctoral researcher
in theoretical condensed-matter physics at Aalto University’s Department of Applied Physics. The position is part of a Research Council of Finland fellowship project led by Dr. Tobias Wolf and focuses on interaction-driven phases in two-dimensional quantum materials.
Project Description
The doctoral researcher will investigate how electronic interactions give rise to collective phases in low-dimensional quantum materials. Systems of interest include rhombohedral multilayer graphene and moiré materials, which offer ways to study how competing orders, unconventional superconductivity, and topological phases emerge and how they can be distinguished experimentally. The researcher will develop microscopic models and use analytical and computational many-body methods suited to the scientific questions and their interests. The doctoral researcher will be part of the Correlated Quantum Materials (CQM) group led by Prof. José Lado and work under the supervision of RCF Fellow Dr. Tobias Wolf.
Requirements
We seek a motivated candidate with:
A master’s degree in physics or a closely related field (by the start of the PhD).
A solid foundation in quantum mechanics and condensed-matter physics.
Programming experience and an interest in combining theory and computation and connecting results to experiments.
Experience with many-body theory or numerical simulations is beneficial but not required.
Proficiency in English (Finnish is not required).
The Position Offers
Full-time, 4-year PhD funding through the Research Council of Finland. The position of Doctoral Researcher is initially filled for 2 years. The contract is continued for another 2 years after a successful midterm review.
Access to world-class computational resources Aalto Triton and Lumi and collaborative research environment at Aalto University.
Opportunities to collaborate with leading international groups (both theoretical and experimental groups).
Travel funding for international conferences and workshops.
A research visit to an international research group as part of the PhD project.
The annual workload of research and teaching staff at Aalto University is 1612 hours. Aalto University follows the salary system of Finnish universities. The starting salary is approximately 3142 €/month (gross), and it increases as the Doctoral Researcher progresses in the research and studies. The contract includes Aalto University occupational healthcare.
