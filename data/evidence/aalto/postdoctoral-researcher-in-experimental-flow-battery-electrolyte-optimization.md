At Aalto University, curiosity moves across disciplines, roles and communities. We bring people together to ask new questions, challenge familiar thinking and turn ideas into impact.
Our work is rooted in responsibility, courage and collaboration, and in a shared commitment to shaping a more sustainable future.
The School of Chemical Engineering is inviting applications for a
Postdoctoral Researcher in experimental flow battery electrolyte optimization
for a two-year fixed-term position for the research group of
Electrolytic Processes
headed by Prof. Pekka Peljo. The position is funded by EU Horizon Europe project FLOWBOOST, where our part focuses on developing high-throughput experimental tools for optimization and characterization of flow battery electrolytes, as well as some synthesis of metal complexes. The project includes 14 partners, and the work requires close collaboration with some of them. You have the ability and willingness to drive high-profile, interdisciplinary research in a joint effort between experimental and computational / theory research. This project requires development of software and hardware for realizing high throughput measurements as well as Byesian optimization as the function of electrolyte composition etc.
You will join the exceptional scientific environment of
Department of Chemistry and Materials Science
hosting two other electrochemistry groups (Prof. Kallio and Yerga) and computational chemistry group specializing in modelling of electrochemical systems (Prof. Laasonen). We also have a wide international collaboration network.
You will conduct experimental scientific research aiming at high impact scientific publications in the fields of electro-chemical automatization and flow battery research. The group’s research focuses on the development of energy storage technologies, experimental electrochemistry, flow batteries, and the modeling of electrochemical processes, for example using the finite element method. Research methodology is experimental electro-chemistry, realized via robotic platform. Additionally, synthesis of some metal complexes as flow battery electrolyte candidates will be required.
What you bring
To thrive in this role, you will need:
an excellent, recently obtained (max 5 years), PhD degree in, for example, Chemistry or related field (Material Science or Physics)
active approach, diligence and cooperation skills
experience in laboratory work
experience in programming (basics is sufficient as AI tools allow advanced programming at a low threshold)
expertise or interest in hardware and electronics (Arduino platforms etc.)
strong aptitude for research of automated electrochemistry
We also value:
expertise in electrochemistry
expertise in organic and inorganic synthesis
Language requirements:
English: Excellent skills in English
Finnish: Finnish language skills are not required
We know that meaningful work is built by people with different backgrounds, experiences and perspectives. If you recognize yourself in the purpose of this role, we encourage you to apply and tell us what you would bring to our community.

About Aalto University
Aalto University builds on the legacy of three leading Finnish universities: Helsinki University of Technology, Helsinki School of Economics and the University of Art and Design Helsinki. Since 2010, we have brought science, art, technology and business together in new ways.
Today, the Aalto community consists of 16,000 students and 5,200 employees, including 446 professors. Half of our faculty members have an international background. Our campus in Espoo is a vibrant cluster of research and innovation with striking architecture, peaceful nature, open spaces for collaboration, and a wide range of services, all with excellent public transport connections.
