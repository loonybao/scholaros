Electronics Integration and Reliability (EILB) (
Electronics Integration and Reliability | Aalto University
) is a research group at Aalto University School of Electrical Engineering. We advance next‑generation electronics through innovative materials, device design, and bonding technologies. By leveraging high‑quality AlN and high‑Al‑content AlGaN, distributed polarization doping, and advanced packaging and interconnection concepts, we develop solutions for cutting‑edge power electronics based on wide‑bandgap (WBG) and ultra‑wide‑bandgap (UWBG) semiconductors, as well as for RF applications and smart sensors. Our mission is to enable miniaturized, sustainable, and energy-efficient systems with superior power handling, higher breakdown voltages, and enhanced high temperature performance.
We are looking for a
doctoral researcher
to advance miniaturised, high‑density heterogeneous integration of WBG and UWBG power electronics through novel high‑temperature interconnects—including solid–liquid interdiffusion (SLID), hybrid and diffusion bonding, and sintering technologies—in collaboration with our international research partners. The research will investigate how interconnect interfacial compatibility, metallurgy and microstructures, and local interface states and defects influence electrical and thermal performance, and long‑term reliability of power electronic modules. These innovative solutions necessitate profound requirement specifications and co-design of thermal, thermomechanical, and microstructural aspects, based on a deep understanding of the electrical requirements of WBG/UWBG semiconductors. The goal is to design, manufacture, and characterise reliable, highly integrated, ultra‑high‑power‑density power modules [~50 kW/L (or kg)] or “power chiplets”. These power modules incorporate innovative control and sensing functions by utilising novel interconnection technologies, which are developed by utilizing advanced materials characterisation methods, electrical and thermal measurements, and multiphysics modelling.
Requirements
M.Sc. or equivalent degree in a suitable area (e.g. electrical engineering, physics or materials science)
good knowledge and understanding of WBG and UWBG power semiconductor technologies
experience in multiphysics simulation tools such as COMSOL, ANSYS and circuit design tools like SPICE or PLEX
experience of working in a cleanroom
experience or genuine interest in advanced microfabrication, novel interconnection and material characterization methods
experience or genuine interest in electronics reliability assessment and failure analysis tools
strong analytical and writing skills

About Aalto University
Aalto University builds on the legacy of three leading Finnish universities: Helsinki University of Technology, Helsinki School of Economics and the University of Art and Design Helsinki. Since 2010, we have brought science, art, technology and business together in new ways.
Today, the Aalto community consists of 16,000 students and 5,200 employees, including 446 professors. Half of our faculty members have an international background. Our campus in Espoo is a vibrant cluster of research and innovation with striking architecture, peaceful nature, open spaces for collaboration, and a wide range of services, all with excellent public transport connections.
