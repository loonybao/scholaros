The
Sustainable Systems
research group in the
Department of Chemical and Metallurgical Engineering
at Aalto University is seeking a highly motivated
DOCTORAL RESEARCHER
to focus on the sustainability of Power-to-X (PtX) value chains.
Are you passionate about advancing renewable energy technologies? Do you want to develop cutting-edge computational frameworks that shape the future of sustainable energy? Join us in solving key challenges in decarbonizing hard-to-abate sectors.
Project Overview
This PhD project aims to develop decision-making models and optimization strategies for the cross-sector integration and operation of PtX technologies. You will work on ensuring these pathways operate within planetary boundaries while meeting stringent criteria for cost-effectiveness, safety, and operational resilience.
Key Research Questions:
How can the intermittent nature of renewable electricity production be economically decoupled from further chemical processing of eFuels and eChemicals?
How can the technical, economic, and environmental feasibility of the PtX value chain be maximized under operational and market uncertainty?
How can an efficient and intelligent computational framework be developed to optimally design and operate low-carbon energy transition pathways for the industrial sectors in Finland and beyond?
Your Role and Responsibilities
Push the flexibility limits of energy transition pathways by modeling operational dynamics, intermediate storage, and variable feedstocks.
Formulate Techno-Economic and Life Cycle-driven Mixed-Integer Linear Programming (MILP) models to optimize full PtX value chains.
Engage with internal group members, academic collaborators, and external industry stakeholders.
Present research outcomes at international academic conferences and publish in high-impact, peer-reviewed journals.
To support your research, you will have access to our group’s comprehensive model library covering PtX technologies, unit operations, training materials, and sustainability assessment frameworks.
Your network and team
You will join the
Sustainable Systems group
, led by
Prof. Rofice Dickson
. Our group focuses on developing mathematical models, algorithms, and computer-aided software tools to explore circular and sustainable solutions for energy systems.
You will be directly supervised by Prof. Rofice Dickson and work alongside a vibrant, multidisciplinary team of PhD students, postdoctoral researchers, and industrial partners.
The unique competitive edge of the
Department of Chemical and Metallurgical Engineering
in the School of Chemical Engineering is based on sustainable utilization of raw materials, designing more efficient processes, and developing new materials and products. Our department’s areas of expertise enable a sustainable future with high-performance products and processes in the circular economy and bioeconomy. The department is in a key position in research of chemical engineering, materials technology, metals processing, efficient energy technologies and digitalization of these. Our core competences include unit operations and processes in chemical engineering, hydro- and pyro-metallurgy, catalyst and polymeric materials, novel catalytic process, process control and process systems engineering.
Your Experience and Ambitions
We are seeking highly motivated candidates with a strong research background in
Process Systems Engineering of energy systems
.
Required:
Master’s Degree: In Chemical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Energy Systems Engineering, or a closely related field.
Optimization background: Solid experience applying mathematical programming (e.g., MILP, MINLP) to energy or process systems.
Programming proficiency: Demonstrated skills in optimization or modeling environments (e.g., GAMS, Python, Julia, or MATLAB).
Desirable (Considered an Advantage):
Assessment Methodologies: Knowledge of TEA and LCA methodologies.
Software tools: Familiarity with process simulation tools (Aspen Plus/Hysys, PRO/II) and LCA software (SimaPro, openLCA).
Research focus: Strong interest in renewable energy pathways, cross-sectoral integration, and decision-making under uncertainty.

The starting date is January 1, 2027, or as mutually agreed. We offer a fixed term contract initially for 2 years.
