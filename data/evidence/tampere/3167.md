Within the
unit of Automation Technology and Mechanical Engineering
,
faculty of Engineering and Natural Sciences
, we are recruiting one (1) postdoctoral researcher position. The unit has about 20 professors and 100 research staff in the areas of Robotics, Automation, and Mechanical engineering. Mechanical engineering is one of the core areas of education and research of the faculty. The position is opened in the Machine Design group (
Machine Dynamics Group (Machine Design) | Tampere University
).
Our group concentrates on machine dynamics, contact modelling, vibration experimental and numerical railway vehicle dynamic analysis that can be used for model-based condition monitoring of different machine system.
Job description
We are looking for one (1) Postdoctoral Research Fellow in mechanical engineering and/or civil engineering. You will focus on the research of Monitoring, Mechanism Analysis, and Mitigation for Sustainable Asset Management of Railway Wheel Out-of-Roundness.  You will collaborate closely with Finnish train maintenance company, VR Fleetcare. You will work one year at Tampere University and another year at VR Fleetcare. At the same time, you will have opportunity to test your technology on full-scale wheel-rail lab (
Full-Scale Wheel-Rail Lab | Tampere University
) at Tampere University and real train owned by VR Fleetcare.
In addition, activities include supervision of PhD / MSc students, preparation of grants and support of the general activities of the research labs.
Requirements
We seek a motivated and self-guided person with strong theoretical background who is interested working on wheel-rail contact mechanism and vibration analysis, motivating to closely collaborate with railway industry and work on the laboratory and field measurements.  If you have background in railway engineering, it will be a plus for this position.
Following background are needed:
Vibration and data analysis of the measurement (mainly accelerometer).
Finite element analysis especially thermomechanical, fatigue and contact mechanism analysis (wheel-rail contact mechanism will be preferable).
Multibody dynamics (railway dynamics will be preferable).
You should have a PhD degree in a relevant field. We expect a competence to pursue independent scientific work and adequate teaching skills. You should be fluent in spoken and written English.
We offer
The position will be offered for a fixed-term period of 2 years. The starting date is expected between Oct. 2026 and Dec. 2026 (or as mutually agreed). A trial period of six (6) months applies to all our new employees.

Tekniikan ja luonnontieteiden tiedekunnan Automaatioteknologian ja konetekniikan yksikkö hakee postdoc-tutkijaa Koneensuunnittelun tutkimusryhmään. Tutkimusryhmä keskittyy koneiden dynamiikkaan, kosketusmallinnukseen, värähtelyiden kokeelliseen ja numeeriseen analyysiin sekä rautatiekaluston dynamiikkaan. Tehtävänkuvaus
Haemme mekaniikan tai rakennustekniikan alan postdoc-tutkijaa tutkimaan rautatiepyörien ympyrämäisyyspoikkeamien (Wheel Out-of-Roundness, OOR) valvontaa, mekanismien analysointia ja lieventämiskeinoja kestävän omaisuudenhallinnan näkökulmasta.
