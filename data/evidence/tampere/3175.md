From wastewater to data-driven epidemic prediction
Background of Project DC9
The COVID-19 pandemic demonstrated how public health measures and changes in population behaviour can influence viral circulation. Following the lifting of restrictions, many viruses showed unusual rebounds and altered seasonal patterns, but these effects remain poorly understood across the broader virome. Wastewater-based epidemiology (WBE), combined with viral enrichment and high-throughput sequencing, provides a powerful approach for monitoring community-level virus circulation across a broad range of pathogens. This doctoral project will integrate wastewater virome data with epidemiological and other population-level data to investigate viral dynamics during and after the COVID-19 pandemic. The study is based on a unique longitudinal dataset of monthly wastewater samples collected from Finnish cities. The project will move from wastewater virome monitoring to data-driven models for epidemic prediction and public health action. By combining comprehensive viral surveillance with advanced data analysis and predictive modelling, the research will provide new insights into viral epidemiology and contribute to the development of wastewater-based early-warning systems for future epidemics and pandemics.
Job Description and Research Objectives:
At Tampere University, DETECTIVE is looking for a Doctoral Candidate 9 to work on Work Package 2.
As Doctoral Candidate 9, you will:
– Build a longitudinal wastewater virome surveillance system
– Apply, qPCR and capture enrichment and high-throughput sequencing to profile all vertebrate-infecting viruses, including human and potential zoonotic pathogens.
– Assess relationships between wastewater viral loads and clinical indicators, including seasonality, early surges, and viral interference.
– Create bioinformatic pipelines for sensitive detection and full-genome recovery
– Develop data-driven models for epidemic prediction, vaccination strategy, and integration of multi-virus WBE into public-health early-warning systems.

Haemme
väitöskirjatutkijaa
DETECTIVE-hankkeeseen (Data-driven Excellence in Training of Epidemiological, Clinical, Technological and Innovative Virus Experts), joka on Horizon Europe -ohjelman Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions (MSCA) Doctoral Network -verkosto. Verkosto kokoaa yhteen eurooppalaisia akateemisia, kliinisiä, kansanterveyden ja teollisuuden toimijoita kouluttamaan uuden sukupolven asiantuntijoita epidemioiden ennakointiin, uusien virusten seurantaan ja pandemioihin varautumiseen.
Tampereen yliopistossa toteutettavassa väitöskirjaprojektissa tutkitaan virusten väestötason kiertoa ja epidemioiden dynamiikkaa COVID-19-pandemian aikana ja sen jälkeen. Projektissa kartoitetaan jätevesien viromia hyödyntämällä qPCR-menetelmiä, virusrikastusta ja uuden sukupolven sekvensointia. Tavoitteena on tunnistaa ja seurata kaikkia selkärankaisia tartuttavia viruksia, mukaan lukien ihmisten taudinaiheuttajat ja mahdolliset zoonoottiset virukset. Jätevesiviromidata yhdistetään epidemiologiseen ja muuhun väestötason tietoon, ja hankkeessa kehitetään bioinformatiikan menetelmiä sekä dataohjattuja malleja epidemioiden ennustamiseen ja kansanterveyden varhaisvaroitusjärjestelmien kehittämiseen.
