We are looking for one or two
Doctoral Researchers
(PhD students)
to develop new theories and methodologies for Deep Learning.
The research scope is intentionally open. The specific research topic will be selected jointly with each successful applicant based on:
the scientific importance and potential impact of the topic,
its relevance to emerging directions in Machine Learning and related domains (including Computer Vision, Robot Perception, AIoT, Neuromorphic Computing, etc.)
the applicant’s background, strengths, and research interests, and
its fit with the research activities of the Computational Intelligence group and the broader ELLIS research community.
The positions are funded by
ELLIS Institute Finland
, which places Fundamental Machine Learning research at the core of its activities and connects this research with applications and societal impact. The successful applicants will join the Computational Intelligence group led by Professor Alexandros Iosifidis at Tampere University.
The positions are full-time and are intended to support the completion of a doctoral degree over four years. They will start on 1 February 2027, or as soon as possible thereafter by mutual agreement. The workplace is located at the Hervanta Campus of Tampere University, Finland.
The Doctoral Researchers will also interact with ELLIS Institute Finland and the wider ELLIS community. ELLIS is a pan-European network of excellence in machine learning and AI that supports international collaboration and the development of researchers across Europe.
JOB DESCRIPTION
The Doctoral Researchers will conduct ambitious research on the
theoretical foundations and methodological development of deep learning models
.
The research may address, but is not limited to:
learning principles and theoretical foundations of deep models,
representation learning and self-supervised learning,
optimization and training dynamics of deep neural networks,
generalization, robustness, adaptation, and uncertainty,
efficient, structured, modular, or interpretable deep learning,
multimodal, generative, or foundation models,
continual, collaborative, or distributed learning,
deep learning for scientific, physical, or embodied systems, and
other high-impact or emerging topics in machine learning.
These examples are not intended to define fixed projects. Applicants are encouraged to propose research questions that they consider important, timely, and scientifically ambitious.
The final research topic will be developed jointly with the selected applicant. We particularly value topics that can lead to substantial methodological or theoretical contributions rather than the direct application of established models to a new dataset.
The Doctoral Researchers will:
formulate original research questions,
develop and analyze new deep learning methods and models,
conduct theoretically or empirically rigorous research,
publish their results in leading international conferences and journals,
present their work at international scientific events,
collaborate with members of the Computational Intelligence group, external research partners of the group, and ELLIS Institute Finland, and
contribute to the group’s research environment and scientific activities.
The research will aim at publication in internationally leading venues relevant to the topic. Depending on the research area, these may include venues recognized by ELLIS, such as NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, AISTATS, COLT, JMLR, AAAI, IJCAI, CVPR, ICCV, ECCV, CoRL, and IEEE Transactions on Pattern Analysis and Machine Intelligence. ELLIS maintains an
approved list of top-tier publication venues across machine-learning-driven fields
.
In addition, the duties of the doctoral researchers may include some teaching tasks (5-10%) in topics connected to their expertise.
REQUIREMENTS
Applicants must have:
A completed Master’s degree in Computer Science, Machine Learning, Artificial Intelligence, Computer Engineering, Applied Mathematics, Statistics, Signal Processing, Robotics, or another relevant field.
Strong knowledge of Machine Learning and Deep Learning methods.
A solid mathematical foundation in relevant areas, such as linear algebra, probability, statistics, optimization, numerical methods, or theoretical computer science.
Strong programming skills, preferably in Python.
Experience with at least one major deep learning framework, such as PyTorch, JAX, or TensorFlow.
The ability to conduct systematic and reproducible scientific research.
The ability to formulate research questions, critically evaluate existing approaches, and develop original solutions;
Very good written and spoken English.
The ability to work both independently and collaboratively in an international research environment.
Because the research scope is open, we do not expect every applicant to have expertise in all the areas listed in the job description. Applicants will be selected according to the overall strength of their profile and their potential to conduct internationally competitive research.
The selected applicants will need to apply for and be granted a study right for a doctoral degree in the doctoral programme of Computing and Electrical Engineering at Tampere University. Please visit
the admission web page of the doctoral programme of Computing and Electrical Engineering
for more information on eligibility requirements of the doctoral programme.
Please note that if you have not completed a university degree in Finland, an official English language certificate will also be required for the admission to the doctoral programme.
