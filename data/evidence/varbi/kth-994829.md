Researcher in scientific and molecular visualization
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
Job description
PDC is seeking a new member to join its team of research software engineers. The successful candidate will become part of the Scientific Services Group, which is tasked to develop software, and services, enabling research which can streamline data analysis and workflows for scientists at KTH and across Sweden.
In this role, the applicant will be responsible for the development and maintenance of
VIAMD
, a tool for visualizing and interactively analyzing chemistry simulations. Additionally, they will lead collaboration efforts with researchers at the
Wallenberg Wood Science Center (WWSC)
and support molecular dynamics simulations of materials studied at WWSC.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree in scientific molecular visualization.
Advanced experience in software programming languages like C/C++
Long experience in developing 3D graphic applications with tools like OpenGL, OpenCL, CUDA.
Good communication skills with users to inform them of our services.
Good knowledge of the English language, as it is required in day to day work.
Knowledge of diversity and equal treatment issues with a particular focus on gender equality.
Preferred qualifications
The applicant should have good connections with swedish network of users that today make use of swedish supercomputer resources, and within networks focusing on molecular visualization.
We consider it favorable if the applicant has a broad knowledge of programming languages commonly used in this particular area today.
Experience of developing and leading visualization project will be viewed as favorable.
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
