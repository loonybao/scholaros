Postdoc in Sustainable Materials, Electrochemical Systems
Login and apply
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Engineering Sciences
Job description
The Department of Applied Physics is seeking a researcher in sustainable materials engineering, electrochemical technologies, and environmental process innovation. The position is placed in an internationally recognized research environment focused on developing advanced materials and processes for water purification, resource recovery, renewable energy conversion, and sustainable chemical production.
You will conduct research on functional nanomaterials and electrochemical systems for applications including capacitive deionization, advanced oxidation processes, photocatalysis, photo-Fenton water treatment, green hydrogen generation, and sustainable ammonia synthesis. The research will involve understanding charge transport, interfacial phenomena, catalytic mechanisms, and structure-property-performance relationships in nanostructured materials.
The work includes the design and optimization of materials and reactors using advanced characterization methods, data-driven approaches and AI-assisted workflows. Emphasis will be placed on accelerating the discovery of next-generation materials and processes that contribute to the transition toward a circular and sustainable society.
The duties also include supervising students and other researchers, collaborating with academic and industrial partners in Sweden and internationally, actively pursuing external research funding, and contributing to the scientific development of the group and department. Teaching and pedagogical activities may form part of the employment.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree in Materials Science, Applied Physics, Chemical Engineering, Chemistry, Nanotechnology, or related disciplines. This eligibility requirement must be met no later than the time the employment decision is made.
Documented research expertise in sustainable materials, electrochemical systems, environmental technologies, photocatalysis, or renewable energy conversion demonstrated through publications in leading international journals.
Experience in developing advanced materials and nanostructures for water, energy, or environmental applications.
Excellent command of English, spoken and written, as the work is conducted in an international research environment.
As a person, you are creative, independent, and highly motivated, with excellent collaborative and interdisciplinary skills
Great emphasis will be placed on scientific excellence, innovation potential, and the ability to translate sustainable technology to industrial applications.
Preferred qualifications
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree, obtained within the last three years prior to the application deadline
Demonstrated research experience in capacitive deionization, membrane-free desalination, water purification technologies, or resource recovery systems.
Experience with photocatalytic and photo-Fenton processes for environmental remediation and water treatment.
Research experience in electrocatalysis, green hydrogen production, carbon-neutral fuels, or sustainable ammonia synthesis.
Knowledge of charge-transfer mechanisms, interfacial electrochemistry, transport phenomena, and catalyst design.
Experience supervising doctoral students, master's students, or junior researchers.
Strong communication skills and demonstrated ability to translate research outcomes into societal and industrial impact.
Experience in leadership, project coordination, innovation activities, and industrial collaboration.
Awareness of diversity and equal opportunity issues, with specific focus on gender equality
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
