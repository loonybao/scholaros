Postdoc in Robot Learning for Manipulation
Login and apply
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
Job description
The postdoctoral researcher will work on robot learning for manipulation, exploring state-of-the-art Vision–Language–Action (VLA) models for tabletop, mobile, or dexterous manipulation. A key focus is leveraging whole-body motion and/or dexterous, multi-fingered hands to improve perception and control, enabling robots to solve complex, contact-rich tasks that require coordinated base, arm, and hand behaviors in dynamic environments. We seek candidates with a strong background in robotics and machine learning, and demonstrated experience in at least two of the following areas: deep learning, reinforcement learning, imitation learning, robot perception, navigation, grasping, and dexterous or mobile manipulation. Experience in any of the following is especially valued: whole-body control, humanoid or multi-DOF platforms, multi-fingered hands, tactile sensing, or learning-based manipulation in simulation and/or on real systems.
