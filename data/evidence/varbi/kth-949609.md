Postdoc in AI-native Wireless Networks
Login and apply
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
Job description
In recent years, Artificial Intelligence (AI) technologies have been developed drastically to change our society and industry. For instance, the performance of wireless networks has been greatly boosted by AI. With increasing data volume and model sizes, high-performance network is also a key enabling technology of advanced AI applications. Wireless network is therefore becoming AI native.
The project will investigate key technologies of AI-enhanced wireless networks and also advanced AI technologies within networks. You will work on the project of inter-disciplinary of AI and wireless networks in terms of theory and algorithm development. Concrete examples include optimized deployment of AI models in networks or optimized wireless networks by AI such as semantic communications or AI based optimization.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree. This eligibility requirement must be met no later than the time the employment decision is made.
You must have a strong track-record of research outputs in the areas of AI and/or wireless networks, shown by high-quality publications in top journals or top AI conferences.
You should have strong backgrounds in communication/information theory, optimization, or AI models (e.g., large language models, reinforcement learning).
Required knowledge/skill include excellent writing and speaking English, as it required in the daily work.
We prefer that you have the excellent capability of collaboration and project management.
Preferred qualifications
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree, obtained within the last three years prior to the application deadline
Knowledge and research experience in Large Language Model
Research expertise
Teaching abilities
Awareness of diversity and equal opportunity issues, with specific focus on gender equality
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
