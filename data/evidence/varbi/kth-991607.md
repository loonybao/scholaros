Postdoc in urban and regional studies, ABE, KTH
Login and apply
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Architecture and Built Environment
Job description
Would you like to contribute to research that can help make urban parks safer and more secure? We are seeking a postdoctoral researcher for a two-year position within a research project funded by FORMAS. Together with the project leaders, you will investigate safety and security in a park in Stockholm, before and after an intervention. The work includes surveys, conduct both digitally and face-to-face, interviews, focus groups, and GIS analysis. You will engage with park users, including vulnerable groups, and organise citizen dialogues with local stakeholders and civil society organisations. An important part of the role is coordinating different groups, including vulnerable groups, collecting and analysing data, and contributing to reports in Swedish and scientific publications in English. Teaching can be part of the work, uptil 20% of full time. The work will be carried out in close collaboration with the project leaders, while also requiring a strong ability to work independently.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree, or a foreign degree considered equivalent to a doctoral degree, in urban and regional planning or a related field, such as human geography, sociology, or criminology. The degree should have been awarded within the last three years. This eligibility requirement must be met no later than the time the employment decision is made.
Experience in independently planning and conducting surveys, interviews, and focus groups.
Experience working with databases and data management, using Excel, SPSS, or similar software.
Good knowledge and demonstrated experience of spatial analysis using GIS.
Documented proficiency in spoken and written Swedish and English.
Strong organisational and coordination skills, with the ability to independently plan and conduct data collection, fieldwork, and other project activities.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills in both Swedish and English, as this will be required in day-to-day work.
Preferred qualifications
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree, obtained within the last three years prior to the application deadline
Experience working with external partners, such as municipalities, the private sector, or civil society organisations.
Demonstrated ability to work independently and systematically based on project objectives and assigned tasks.
Strong subject-specific expertise relevant to the research project.
Awareness of diversity and equal treatment, with particular attention to gender equality.
Strong collaborative and teamwork skills.
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
