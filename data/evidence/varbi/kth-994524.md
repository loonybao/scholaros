Postdoc in Information Theoretic Analysis of Machine Learning Methods
Login and apply
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science
Job description
We are seeking a postdoctoral researcher to strengthen our research environment and to contribute to fundamental research on the theoretical foundations of machine learning and decision-making. Your primary goal in this role will be to develop your research profile through both independent work and collaboration with colleagues in the department.
The position is based in the Department of Information Science and Engineering within the School of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science.
Qualifications
Requirements
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree. This eligibility requirement must be met no later than the time the employment decision is made.
A strong background in information theory and learning theory.
Excellent academic track record and potential as well as well-developed analytical and problem-solving skills.
Documented track record of successful research grant applications.
Ability to work independently.
Excellent skills in English, both oral and in writing
as this will be required in day-to-day work.
Preferred qualifications
A doctoral degree or an equivalent foreign degree, obtained within the last three years prior to the application deadline
Awareness of diversity and equal opportunity issues, with specific focus on gender equality
Great emphasis will be placed on personal skills.
