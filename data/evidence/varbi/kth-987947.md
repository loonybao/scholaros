Researcher in Environmentally Extended Multi-Regional Input-Output Analysis
Apply for position
KTH Royal Institute of Technology, School of Architecture and Built Environment
Job description
The researcher will work with Environmentally Extended Multi-Regional Input-Output Analysis (MRIO) especially focusing on developing and applying methods for prospective studies. The overall aim of the projects is to describe how environmental impacts from consumption can be reduced to sustainable levels. The successful applicant will work in several projects together with researchers and PhD students from KTH, Luxembourg Institute of Science and Technology (LIST) and Statistics Sweden (SCB). The work may include writing applications for further work, presentations at international conferences, interaction with stakeholders, visit to LIST, teaching and supervision of students. The work will be performed at the Division of Sustainability Assessment and Management at the Department of Sustainable Development, Environmental Science and Engineering at KTH, a multidisciplinary department where engineers, and natural and social scientist are working together to understand and develop solutions for a more sustainable society.
